### Main Topic: AI Strategy

**Main Thesis:** AI companies use vague public statements to appeal to both safety concerns and accelerationists, allowing them to pursue superintelligence without making concrete commitments.

**Simple Summary:** AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI use vague language about pausing AI development to satisfy different groups—those worried about risks and those pushing for speed. This strategy allows them to signal safety concerns to the public while still accelerating the development of superintelligent AI. The article argues that this is a deliberate way to manage public opinion, similar to how old industries hid the dangers of their products.

**The Bottom Line:** > Despite the vague talk about pauses, the companies are continuing to pursue superintelligence at full speed, meaning the public should focus on their actions rather than their carefully worded statements.

### Main Topic: Oil Supply

**Main Thesis:** The fuel crisis continues even if the war ends, and the United States has the ability to control oil exports.

**Simple Summary:** There is an ongoing fuel shortage caused by the disruption in oil supply. Even if the conflict stops, the supply problems will remain for a long time. Because the U.S. currently exports more oil than it imports, the government has the power to restrict exports if needed.

**The Bottom Line:** > The ongoing fuel shortage is a persistent problem, and the U.S. holds significant leverage over the global oil supply.

### Main Topic: Smartphones and births

**Main Thesis:** The introduction of smartphones helped change people's behavior by reducing face-to-face contact, which contributed to a decline in US birth rates.

**Simple Summary:** Studies suggest that after 2007, the rise of smartphones caused people to interact less in person. This change in behavior is thought to have played a role in the decline of the birth rate in the United States.

**The Bottom Line:** > Increased digital communication and less in-person contact may be linked to lower birth rates.

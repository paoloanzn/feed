### Main Topic: Private Funding
**Main Thesis:** A company can use a system of private voting to secretly determine which academic papers are most promising for funding new projects.

**Simple Summary:** A biotech company wants to find the best research papers to fund new projects, but they don't want their competitors to know their strategy. The system involves asking a group of people to privately vote or assign value (tokens) to different research papers. The company then publicly announces which projects they are considering, and the people who voted privately are paid based on how well their choices matched the company's final decision.

**The Bottom Line:** > This method allows a company to make private, informed funding decisions based on expert judgment while keeping their competitive strategy secret.

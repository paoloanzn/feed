### Main Topic: Meeting Automation

**Main Thesis:** The author attempted to automate phone notifications for meetings but found no reliable solution, so they resorted to manually setting timers.

**Simple Summary:** The author wanted their phone to alert them before meetings so they wouldn't miss them, but they struggled to set up automatic alerts. After trying various automation tools, they gave up and started manually setting timers for each meeting instead.

**The Bottom Line:** > While automating these alerts would save time, the author realized that manually setting timers is a simple, albeit inefficient, workaround for the problem.

### Main Topic: Public Transit Economics

**Main Thesis:** Free public transportation caused massive frustration and logistical chaos because the system failed to use economic principles to manage demand and allocate resources.

**Simple Summary:** During the NFL Draft, free bus rides led to huge crowds trying to leave, causing traffic jams and unrest. The author argues that if the public transit system had charged a reasonable fare, people would have been less likely to crowd the buses and would have used other transportation options, which would have reduced congestion. The failure was in the public sector's inability to anticipate and manage demand using economic logic.

**The Bottom Line:** > Public services should be managed like a private business, using pricing to manage demand and ensure efficient use of resources rather than relying on free services that cause chaos.

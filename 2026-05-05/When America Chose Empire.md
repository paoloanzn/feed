### Main Topic: American Imperialism

**Main Thesis:** The shift from founding ideals of voluntary consent to the reality of empire drove the United States toward an aggressive, interventionist, and war-focused government.

**Simple Summary:** The article uses the conflict in the Philippines to show how the U.S. moved from believing in voluntary agreements to pursuing empire and conquest. It argues that the American ideal of consent was abandoned as the nation embraced expansionism, driven by figures who believed power and military action were necessary for national greatness. This shift meant the government became more focused on war and intervention rather than peaceful agreement.

**The Bottom Line:** > The pursuit of empire fundamentally changed the American mindset, leading the government to prioritize aggressive power and perpetual war over the original principles of liberty and consent.

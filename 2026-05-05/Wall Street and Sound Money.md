### Main Topic: Free Bus Fares

**Main Thesis:** Making scarce goods free, even for transportation, can cause unexpected chaos for those who rely on them.

**Simple Summary:** City officials made bus fares free to get fans to attend an event. However, this decision caused problems and chaos for the bus riders themselves.

**The Bottom Line:** > Giving away something people need can lead to unintended negative consequences for the people who rely on it.

### Main Topic: Free Bus Fares

**Main Thesis:** Making a necessary service free can cause unexpected chaos for the people who rely on it.

**Simple Summary:** City officials made bus fares free during an NFL draft to encourage fans to attend. However, this action caused problems and chaos for the bus riders themselves.

**The Bottom Line:** > Giving away something people need can create unintended problems for those who depend on it.

### Main Topic: Economic Theory

**Main Thesis:** A new theory about prices misses a crucial, earlier solution provided by Mises's work on money.

**Simple Summary:** Some recent work tries to combine ideas from economists like Menger and Mosler to create a complete theory of prices. However, the author argues that this new theory ignores a foundational idea from Mises, which already solved the main problem in monetary theory.

**The Bottom Line:** > To understand how prices work correctly, you must first understand Mises's earlier rules, as they provide the necessary foundation for all other economic theories.

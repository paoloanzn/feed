### Main Topic: Data Center Policy

**Main Thesis:** Governments should not simply ban or allow data centers; instead, property rights and free market forces should determine where they are built.

**Simple Summary:** Because AI creates a huge demand for data centers, some communities don't want them nearby. The author argues that the best way to solve this problem is not by making blanket rules, but by respecting private property rights and letting the free market decide where these facilities should be located.

**The Bottom Line:** > To manage the placement of data centers effectively, we should rely on market decisions and property rights rather than government mandates.

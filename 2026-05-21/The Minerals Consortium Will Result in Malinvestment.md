**Main Thesis:** Forming a group to secure minerals will cause bad economic investments.

**Simple Summary:** The plan for the U.S. to team up with other countries to get vital minerals for manufacturing is a bad policy. This effort will lead to poor and wasteful investments in the economy.

**The Bottom Line:** > This international plan is flawed and will result in bad economic decisions for the country.

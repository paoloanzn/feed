### Main Topic: Bayesian Machine Learning

**Main Thesis:** The article establishes the mathematical foundation of Bayesian statistics, showing how probability distributions (priors and posteriors) are used to build, estimate, and evaluate machine learning models.

**Simple Summary:** This text explains the Bayesian approach to machine learning, which focuses on updating our beliefs about a model as we collect data. It introduces key concepts like the "prior" (initial belief), the "posterior" (updated belief after seeing data), and the "KL divergence" (a measure of how different two probability distributions are). It then details methods—such as Cross-Validation and WAIC—to accurately measure how good a statistical model is and how well it can generalize to new data.

**The Bottom Line:** > By understanding these mathematical tools, we can accurately measure the accuracy of our models and ensure that our predictions are reliable and generalize well to new situations.

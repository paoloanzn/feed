### Main Topic: Public Concern

**Main Thesis:** People often worry less about the actual threat and more about causing panic when discussing serious risks, which can lead to irrational behavior.

**Simple Summary:** When people talk about serious dangers, like a pandemic or AI risk, they often focus on making sure they don't scare others. This happens because people worry that warning others will cause unnecessary fear or lead them to act irrationally, such as buying too many supplies or protesting poorly. The article suggests that managing public fear sometimes becomes more important than addressing the threat in the most rational way.

**The Bottom Line:** > It is important to recognize that managing public fear can sometimes distract from addressing real dangers, leading to less rational responses to serious risks.

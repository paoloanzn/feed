### Main Topic: Mass Sculpture

**Main Thesis:** The United States cannot successfully revive mass sculpture because it lacks the necessary industry and infrastructure.

**Simple Summary:** An attempt to build many statues in America is impossible because the country does not have the necessary artists, factories, or workers to do it. The article discusses how sculpture has always been suited for mass production and looks at where this tradition is currently being practiced.

**The Bottom Line:** > To create large-scale art, you need the right skills and resources, and other nations are better equipped to handle this kind of production.

### Main Topic: Ignoring Simple Advice

**Main Thesis:** Smart people often ignore obvious life advice because they dismiss it as cliché, but applying these simple principles is essential for a better life.

**Simple Summary:** Many smart people ignore common-sense advice because they think it is obvious or lazy. The article points out several simple, proven habits that everyone should follow, such as believing in yourself, looking for the positive, focusing on the most important tasks (the 80/20 rule), getting enough sleep, and living in the present moment. Ignoring these basic truths leads to inefficiency and unhappiness.

**The Bottom Line:** > Stop dismissing simple advice and start applying these obvious habits, as doing so will lead to a vastly better and more productive life.

### Main Topic: Tax and Spending

**Main Thesis:** Achieving economic prosperity and reducing national debt requires not just cutting taxes, but also controlling government spending.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that while cutting taxes can boost the economy, it is not enough if government spending remains high. Historically, the best results came from policies that reduced both taxes and government spending, as demonstrated by figures like Andrew Mellon. The author suggests that recent tax cuts, without corresponding spending controls, have not led to the desired reduction in national debt, even when the economy is growing.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly improve the economy and reduce debt, governments must combine tax cuts with strict spending controls, rather than relying on tax cuts alone.

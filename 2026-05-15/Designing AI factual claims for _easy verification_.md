### Main Topic: AI Verification

**Main Thesis:** To build trustworthy AI, systems must be designed to prioritize finding and presenting verifiable, cited primary sources rather than generating unverified information.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that because AI can make things up, we need to redesign AI so it always finds and shows the original source for every claim. This involves making AI quote directly from sources, provide easy ways to check those sources, and clearly name the human authors responsible for the information.

**The Bottom Line:** > For AI to be trustworthy, it must stop guessing and start acting like a research librarian, ensuring every piece of information is traceable back to a human-verified original.

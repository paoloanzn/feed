### Main Topic: Political Shifts

**Main Thesis:** The growth of socialist and progressive politics is driven by public desire and dissatisfaction with the current economic system, rather than by the actual success of socialist policies.

**Simple Summary:** Political success is often about what people want to see, not what politicians actually propose. The article argues that the Democratic Party is moving toward the left because of anti-Trump sentiment and the influence of progressive groups. While these policies are ambitious, the real problems in cities and states (like crime and poor services) show that the current system is failing. Ultimately, socialists gain political power because they successfully convince people they care, even though their economic ideas have not yet resulted in real-world success.

**The Bottom Line:** > Political success for left-wing parties is based on convincing voters of shared values and care, not on the economic success of their policies.

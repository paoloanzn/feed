### Main Topic: Inflation Measurement

**Main Thesis:** Different ways of measuring inflation, like Trimmed PCE, are used by government officials to justify policy decisions, but the underlying statistical methods are flawed when dealing with unpredictable human behavior.

**Simple Summary:** The article discusses the debate over how economists measure inflation, specifically focusing on the Trimmed PCE. This method tries to smooth out price changes to find the true trend, but critics argue that this process is manipulated by government officials. The author then argues that the statistical tools used in economics often ignore the unpredictable nature of human choices, meaning that official inflation numbers and interest rate decisions are based on flawed logic rather than objective truth.

**The Bottom Line:** > Economic statistics are often used by leaders to justify policies, but because human actions are unpredictable, these measures do not accurately reflect the real economy or lead to sound decisions.

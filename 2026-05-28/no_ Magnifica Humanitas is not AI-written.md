### Main Topic: AI Detection

**Main Thesis:** We should not rely on AI detection tools to judge the authorship of formal documents, especially when considering the context of historical writing.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues against the idea that the Pope's encyclical was written by AI. They contend that organizations like the Vatican have the resources for human collaboration, and that AI detection tools are unreliable, particularly when analyzing formal writing styles. The author points out that the actual encyclical was written by humans, and that AI detectors often fail to accurately identify human-written, formal text.

**The Bottom Line:** > Do not blindly trust AI detection software when evaluating important documents; human judgment and context are far more reliable indicators of authorship.

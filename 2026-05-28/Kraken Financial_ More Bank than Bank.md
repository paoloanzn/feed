### Main Topic: Banking System Risk

**Main Thesis:** New digital banks operating on a full-reserve basis pose a threat to the Federal Reserve system, which is trying to regulate them to prevent instability.

**Simple Summary:** The article discusses a conflict between traditional banking (fractional-reserve) and newer digital asset banks (full-reserve). The Federal Reserve is concerned that if these new institutions, which hold 100% of their assets, become widespread, they could destabilize the current financial system. To prevent this, the Fed is restricting access for these newer banks to payment systems.

**The Bottom Line:** > The shift toward full-reserve banking is seen as a threat to the financial system, and regulators are taking action to manage this change to ensure stability.

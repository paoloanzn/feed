### Main Topic: Broken Trust

**Main Thesis:** When governments or institutions repeatedly break promises, public trust erodes, which destabilizes markets.

**Simple Summary:** If leaders or systems frequently make deals or statements that turn out to be false, people stop believing them. This loss of trust causes instability in the economy, leading to unpredictable market behavior and eventual collapse.

**The Bottom Line:** > Trust is essential for markets to work; when promises are broken, the resulting lack of faith causes severe economic problems.

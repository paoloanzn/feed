### Main Topic: Brain Augmentation

**Main Thesis:** We can boost human intelligence by training an AI to simulate the patterns of extra neurons, effectively increasing brain mass.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that the easiest way to improve human intelligence is to simulate what the brain would do if it had more processing power. Since we can't easily change our genes right now, we can use powerful computers (GPUs) to train an AI to mimic the activity of extra neurons. This process effectively boosts the brain's capacity, allowing us to extend how much a person can learn and think.

**The Bottom Line:** > We can use AI to simulate brain growth to enhance human intelligence, and figuring out the correct rules for this process is the main challenge.

### Main Topic: Carbon Structure Building

**Main Thesis:** Scientists demonstrated a method to precisely build carbon structures on a surface by controlling how atoms are donated and bonded.

**Simple Summary:** Researchers used special tools to move carbon units from molecules onto a silicon surface. They showed they could control exactly where these units landed and how they chemically bonded, allowing them to step-by-step assemble complex carbon chains.

**The Bottom Line:** > This work proves that controlling how atoms attach is the key to building highly precise and programmable materials at the smallest scale.

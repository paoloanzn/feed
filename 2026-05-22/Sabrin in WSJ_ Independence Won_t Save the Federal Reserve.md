### Main Topic: Federal Reserve

**Main Thesis:** Simply having independence is not enough to fix the Federal Reserve's long history of bad policies.

**Simple Summary:** An expert argues that the main problem with the Federal Reserve is that it has followed harmful policies for over a hundred years. He believes that just making the Fed independent will not solve these deep-seated historical problems.

**The Bottom Line:** > To fix the Federal Reserve, it needs more than just independence; it needs a complete change in its approach to economics.

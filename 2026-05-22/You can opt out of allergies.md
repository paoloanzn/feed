### Main Topic: Allergy Treatment

**Main Thesis:** You can effectively reduce seasonal allergies by using immunotherapy treatments like shots or drops, which significantly reduce suffering.

**Simple Summary:** People can avoid seasonal allergies by undergoing immunotherapy, which involves gradually exposing the body to the allergen. The two main methods are allergy shots (injections) and allergy drops (tablets/drops taken under the tongue). While shots are common in the US and covered by insurance, drops are also effective, though less widely accepted. The author suggests that while the process takes time, the reduction in daily suffering and cognitive benefits makes the treatment worthwhile.

**The Bottom Line:** > If you are suffering from seasonal allergies, considering an immunotherapy treatment is likely worth the time and effort because it significantly reduces discomfort and improves quality of life.

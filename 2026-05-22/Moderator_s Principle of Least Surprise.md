### Main Topic: System Predictability

**Main Thesis:** When designing systems or making decisions, the goal should be to ensure that the outcomes are unsurprising to the people involved.

**Simple Summary:** This article explains the "Principle of Least Surprise," which means that when people interact with a system (like a website) or when moderators make decisions about a community, the results should be clear and expected. It argues that moderators should focus on making things easy for members, and if a major decision surprises people, it signals a mistake in the process. Predictability provides stability and reassurance, especially in communities where people feel anxious about social norms.

**The Bottom Line:** > To create stable and healthy communities, focus on making expectations clear and predictable, even if it means avoiding perfect rules, and always aim for harm reduction.

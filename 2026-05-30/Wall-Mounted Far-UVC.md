### Main Topic: Lighting setup

**Main Thesis:** You can safely and conveniently install a light source by mounting it to the wall and carefully calculating its height and angle.

**Simple Summary:** The author discusses how they set up a far-UV light for events. They found that mounting the light to the wall is safer and more convenient than using a stand. By positioning the light high and angled down, they ensured it met safety limits for people in the room. They also managed the wiring and power setup to make the installation easy and portable.

**The Bottom Line:** > Proper wall mounting and careful placement are essential for safety and efficiency, which saves time when preparing for gatherings.

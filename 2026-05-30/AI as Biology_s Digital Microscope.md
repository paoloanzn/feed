### Main Topic: AI in Biology

**Main Thesis:** Scientists can use special methods to look inside complex AI models to understand the exact biological rules they have learned.

**Simple Summary:** AI models are great at predicting biological structures, but we don't know how they make those predictions. This research developed a method called ProtoMech to act like a digital microscope, allowing scientists to trace the internal workings of these AI models. By doing this, they can uncover the hidden patterns and circuits that govern how proteins function and how they change when mutated.

**The Bottom Line:** > By treating AI as a digital microscope, we can move beyond just predicting outcomes and use these models to truly discover new biological facts and accelerate scientific breakthroughs.

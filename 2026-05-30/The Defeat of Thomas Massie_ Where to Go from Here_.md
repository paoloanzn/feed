### Main Topic: Political Defeat

**Main Thesis:** The defeat of Thomas Massie removes an important voice for free-market and anti-war ideas in the political landscape.

**Simple Summary:** Thomas Massie was a figure who represented free-market and anti-war political ideas. His recent primary defeat means that these specific viewpoints are now less represented in the country's politics. The article asks what should happen next for people who believe in these ideas.

**The Bottom Line:** > The loss of this voice means that those who support free markets and peace need to find new ways to make their ideas heard in government.

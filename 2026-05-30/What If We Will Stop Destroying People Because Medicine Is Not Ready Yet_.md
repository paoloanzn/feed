### Main Topic: Future of Death

**Main Thesis:** If society decides death is unacceptable, it must create new legal and economic systems to preserve people, fundamentally changing how we view life, family, and law.

**Simple Summary:** The article explores a future where society decides that death is not an acceptable outcome, leading to the legal status of "unavailable" people who are preserved rather than buried. This shift requires new laws, economies (like a "Return Fund"), and moral frameworks, forcing society to redefine concepts like ownership, family, and even suicide. The core challenge is figuring out who has the right to preserve people and how to manage the consequences of this new reality.

**The Bottom Line:** > If we choose to preserve people, society must establish strict rules and funding mechanisms now to ensure that this future is fair, legal, and doesn't lead to new forms of injustice.

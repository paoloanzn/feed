### Main Topic: DNA discovery

**Main Thesis:** Scientists discovered that genes are encoded in DNA through experimental work involving bacteria, which established DNA as the fundamental material of heredity.

**Simple Summary:** For a long time, scientists debated whether genes were made of protein or DNA. The breakthrough came from experiments involving bacteria, specifically by Frederick Griffith and Oswald Avery. They showed that a substance (the "transforming principle") passed between bacteria was responsible for hereditary changes. By testing different chemicals, they proved that DNA, not protein, was the material that carried genetic information.

**The Bottom Line:** > This discovery established that DNA is the blueprint for all life on Earth, forming the foundation for all modern biology and medicine.

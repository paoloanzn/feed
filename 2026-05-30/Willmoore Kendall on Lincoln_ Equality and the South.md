### Main Topic: American History

**Main Thesis:** The author warns that focusing solely on the idea of equality can lead to a dangerous cycle of new revolutions and political conflict.

**Simple Summary:** The article examines how the idea that "all men are created equal" has been used throughout American history, especially during the Civil War. It argues that the way this idea is interpreted can lead to a dangerous situation where people ignore the rule of law and self-government. The author suggests that the South was correct to reject the idea that the Civil War was necessary to achieve this equality, warning against future movements that demand constant change in the name of equality.

**The Bottom Line:** > We must be careful not to let the pursuit of equality override the basic limits of government and the principles of self-rule.

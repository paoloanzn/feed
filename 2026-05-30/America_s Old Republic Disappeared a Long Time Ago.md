### Main Topic: American Republic

**Main Thesis:** The current United States is fundamentally different from the republic established by the Founding Fathers.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that the version of America we live in today is not the same republic that was created in the late eighteenth century.

**The Bottom Line:** > The current system is a completely different entity than the original one, which means the old ideas no longer apply to the present.

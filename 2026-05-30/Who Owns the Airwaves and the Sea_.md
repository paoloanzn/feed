### Main Topic: Property Rights

**Main Thesis:** People should be able to own the airwaves and the sea privately, not just the government.

**Simple Summary:** Historically, people believed that things like the airwaves and waterways belonged to the government and were public property. However, some thinkers argue that the idea of private ownership can be applied to these resources as well.

**The Bottom Line:** > Recognizing that private property can apply to natural resources changes how we think about who controls and manages the air and water.

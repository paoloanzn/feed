### Main Topic: Community Introduction

**Main Thesis:** This page explains where new members of the LessWrong community can share their ideas, ask questions, and find introductory material.

**Simple Summary:** This is a guide for newcomers to the LessWrong community. You can use this space to share personal stories, ask how you found the site, suggest new features, or share general ideas. Newcomers can also start by reading the "Highlights" to learn the main ideas of the community.

**The Bottom Line:** > New members should use this space to introduce themselves and read the Highlights to easily get started in the community.

### Main Topic: Nation formation

**Main Thesis:** The Declaration of Independence was not the event that created the American nation; it is a later idea projected backward onto the founding era.

**Simple Summary:** Many people mistakenly believe the Declaration of Independence was the moment the United States was officially formed. The author argues that this view is wrong. The actual process of creating a unified nation happened before the Declaration was written.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding the true timeline of nation-building changes how we view the history of the United States.

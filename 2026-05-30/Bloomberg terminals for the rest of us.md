## Key Arguments & Concepts

The author implies that traditional, linear forecasting often fails because it ignores the feedback loops, emergent properties, and non-linearity inherent in complex systems.

The implicit call is to move away from static models toward dynamic, iterative systems that can absorb new information and adapt.

### C. The Necessity of Iteration and Feedback
The process must be cyclical: **Model $\rightarrow$ Act $\rightarrow$ Observe $\rightarrow$ Refine Model.** This iterative loop is crucial for managing uncertainty.

While AI can process data, the text suggests that the final, critical step—interpreting the model, setting the goals, and deciding which actions to take—remains a fundamentally human, strategic endeavor.

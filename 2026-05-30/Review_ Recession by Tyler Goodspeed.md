### Main Topic: Economic Shocks

**Main Thesis:** Recessions are usually caused by unpredictable external events (shocks) rather than predictable boom-and-bust cycles.

**Simple Summary:** The article reviews a book arguing that economic downturns are not caused by natural boom-and-bust patterns but by unexpected external events, like natural disasters or geopolitical conflicts. The author uses historical data to show that the length of an economic boom does not predict how bad the resulting recession will be, meaning recessions are largely unpredictable. The article also points out that policymakers often make mistakes when trying to manage these events, emphasizing that focusing on avoiding errors is more important than trying to perfectly predict the economy.

**The Bottom Line:** > Economic downturns are driven by unpredictable external shocks, so policymakers should focus on avoiding mistakes rather than trying to perfectly predict or control the business cycle.

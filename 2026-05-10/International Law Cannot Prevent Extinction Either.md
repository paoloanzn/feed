### Main Topic: AI Safety Treaties

**Main Thesis:** International treaties are unlikely to prevent AI extinction because powerful nations ignore them, and the AI race has different incentives than nuclear proliferation.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues against using international law to stop the AI race, pointing out that powerful countries ignore treaties without consequence. They explain that the AI competition is different from nuclear weapons because the incentives are different, and there is no scientific agreement on how dangerous AI is. Furthermore, controlling the necessary computing power (GPUs) is much easier than controlling nuclear materials, making any proposed treaty weak. Instead of waiting for a flawed treaty, the author calls for massively increasing technical safety research.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't rely on international treaties to stop AI risk; instead, focus all efforts on making technical safety research wider, more desperate, and more funded to ensure safety.

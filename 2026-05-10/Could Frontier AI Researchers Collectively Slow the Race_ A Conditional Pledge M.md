### Main Topic: AI Safety Pledge

**Main Thesis:** Researchers propose a system where they can collectively agree to temporarily stop advancing AI capabilities to slow the development race and force public and government attention on AI risks.

**Simple Summary:** Many AI researchers are worried about the dangers of advanced AI, like human extinction or mass manipulation. They suggest a "conditional pledge" where researchers would agree to pause their work if enough peers across different labs did the same. This action would serve to slow down the speed of AI development and highlight the urgency of safety concerns to governments.

**The Bottom Line:** > This mechanism aims to give researchers a concrete way to act on their fears, potentially buying crucial time for safety research and enabling governments to take necessary action to govern AI.

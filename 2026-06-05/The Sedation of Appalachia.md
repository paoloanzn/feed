### Main Topic: Regional Decline

**Main Thesis:** Economic changes in Appalachia caused social breakdowns and increased drug addiction.

**Simple Summary:** Despite having strong communities, the Appalachian region experienced social problems and rising drug use after the welfare state expanded and the coal and steel industries shrank.

**The Bottom Line:** > The decline of the region's main industries directly led to serious social and health problems for the people living there.

### Main Topic: Edo City Control

**Main Thesis:** Japan achieved long-lasting peace by using the city of Edo as a physical system to control and monitor the powerful ruling classes.

**Simple Summary:** The Tokugawa government kept Japan peaceful by concentrating the powerful samurai and regional lords (daimyo) in the city of Edo. Edo was designed like a giant prison, with strict walls and checkpoints, which made the elites live under constant surveillance. This system ensured stability but also created an extremely unequal society where the common people provided resources while the elites were contained.

**The Bottom Line:** > The stability of Tokugawa Japan was achieved through a system that trapped the powerful in a controlled city, showing how government control can shape both peace and economic inequality.

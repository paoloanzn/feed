### Main Topic: Economic Theory

**Main Thesis:** Economic data alone is meaningless without a theoretical framework based on understanding human choices and actions.

**Simple Summary:** Most people look at economic numbers like GDP to judge the economy, but the author argues that this is insufficient. True understanding requires knowing that people act purposefully, making choices based on what they have (scarcity of means). Therefore, the real driver of the economy is human action and production (supply), not just consumer demand. To make sense of any data, you must first have a theory about how people behave.

**The Bottom Line:** > Economic data only reports what happened; a theory is needed to explain *why* it happened, meaning that understanding human behavior is more important than just looking at numbers.

### Main Topic: City Development

**Main Thesis:** 19th-century cities grew rapidly by mixing private freedom with government control over essential services and infrastructure.

**Simple Summary:** Cities in the 1800s grew very large and wealthy, but this growth happened because landowners had freedom to build, while the government controlled important things like street layouts and utilities (such as electricity and pipes). These services were run by companies that made money but had to keep prices controlled for the public.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding this system shows how private freedom and public control can work together to create massive urban growth and essential services.

### Main Topic: Time and Economics

**Main Thesis:** Time is a limited resource in economics, and understanding this limitation is crucial for sound economic thought.

**Simple Summary:** Economists who focus on time, like Mises, believe that time is a unique resource that cannot be created or made more of. Because time is limited, it plays a very important role in how we understand the economy.

**The Bottom Line:** > Recognizing that time is a scarce resource helps explain why economic decisions and limits are so important in the real world.

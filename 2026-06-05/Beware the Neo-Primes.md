### Main Topic: Defense Contractors

**Main Thesis:** New high-tech defense companies, despite their advanced claims, are likely to become corrupt and bloated like older companies.

**Simple Summary:** The article warns that the newest group of high-tech defense contractors, called "neo-primes," are not trustworthy. The author argues that these companies, which promise "smart" military applications, will eventually become as corrupt and overly large as other defense firms.

**The Bottom Line:** > Be cautious of new defense companies because history suggests that technological advancement does not prevent corruption and greed.

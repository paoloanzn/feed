### Main Topic: State and War

**Main Thesis:** The root cause of war and conflict is the power of the state, not capitalism or free markets.

**Simple Summary:** Many people misunderstand the relationship between freedom and the economy. This article argues that the U.S. state is not a supporter of capitalism or freedom; instead, the state uses force and taxes to control the economy. The author explains that war and violence come from the structure of these states, not from the free market itself. Therefore, to end war, people must limit or eliminate state power.

**The Bottom Line:** > States, not capitalism, are the source of war and oppression because they hold a monopoly on violence and use taxation to control people. To achieve peace, governments must be limited.

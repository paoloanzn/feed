### Main Topic: Inflation definition

**Main Thesis:** The author is worried that changing how the Federal Reserve defines "inflation" allows for even more inflation to occur.

**Simple Summary:** Even if recent price increases weren't strict inflation, they caused real pain. The author is concerned that the way the Fed measures inflation is being changed, which could justify higher inflation in the future.

**The Bottom Line:** > If the definition of inflation is changed, it could lead to more price increases and economic instability.

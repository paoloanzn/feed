### Main Topic: Research collaboration

**Main Thesis:** Writing your first research paper alone is a poor strategy; instead, you should join a part-time research fellowship to work on a team.

**Simple Summary:** Trying to write a research paper by yourself is difficult because you miss out on crucial elements like getting fast feedback, learning diverse skills, and staying motivated. Working in a team, like a fellowship, allows you to get guidance from experts, learn many different skills, and have external motivation, which makes the process much more effective.

**The Bottom Line:** > To succeed in research and build your career, working with a team and a mentor is much more effective than trying to do everything by yourself.

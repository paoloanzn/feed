### Main Topic: Science and Values

**Main Thesis:** Human beings cannot be properly studied by physical science because they are conscious and make free choices.

**Simple Summary:** Murray Rothbard argues that science fails when it tries to study people because people are conscious and make choices. He shows that many scientific and social ideas are built on contradictions, especially when they try to define concepts like "society" or "public good." Furthermore, he critiques the idea that science is "value-free," pointing out that scientists often hide their personal values by simply adopting what society already believes, which makes their pursuit of truth biased.

**The Bottom Line:** > To understand reality accurately, we must stop treating people as mindless parts of a machine and stop letting social or scientific theories hide their personal values behind claims of objective truth.

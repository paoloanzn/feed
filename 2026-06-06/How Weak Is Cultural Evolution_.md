### Main Topic: Cultural Evolution

**Main Thesis:** Declining birth rates are caused by cultural habits that no longer fit modern life, and the process of cultural change is too slow to correct these problems.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the drop in world fertility is not a natural biological decline but a cultural problem. It suggests that old habits, like those related to status, freedom, and living arrangements, are now causing people to make choices that are harmful. Because cultural change is much slower now, these harmful habits are harder to fix, leading to widespread maladaptive trends.

**The Bottom Line:** > Modern cultural trends are misaligned with biological reality, and the slow pace of cultural change means we are stuck in these harmful patterns for much longer than we used to be.

### Main Topic: War Strategy

**Main Thesis:** How a group decides to fight a war determines whether they win or lose.

**Simple Summary:** The way a group chooses to fight a war is extremely important because it directly affects the final outcome. The choice of strategy—whether fighting as a unified state or as separate entities—is the key factor that determines success or failure in the conflict.

**The Bottom Line:** > Strategic decisions about how a war is fought are critical because they directly control the results of the conflict.

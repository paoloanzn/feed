### Main Topic: Procrastination and Expectancy

**Main Thesis:** Low expectations are caused more by actual capabilities and the structure of a task than by self-confidence, and ignoring real-world unpredictability is the only way to avoid paralysis.

**Simple Summary:** When people procrastinate, it often isn't just a lack of confidence; it's a mismatch between what they think they can do and the actual difficulty of the task. Focusing only on boosting confidence without building real skills can lead to false confidence, which causes problems when reality hits. Furthermore, when facing long, uncertain tasks, assuming that future promises will be kept or the environment will stay the same is dangerous. Since the world changes quickly and unpredictably, the best approach is to accept this reality rather than trying to control it, which stops the cycle of worry and inaction.

**The Bottom Line:** > Stop focusing on fake confidence and accept that the future is unpredictable; this acceptance is the only way to move forward effectively.

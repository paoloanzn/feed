### Main Topic: Political Finance

**Main Thesis:** Democracy is not about the people ruling, but an elite competition where politicians use state power for their own gain.

**Simple Summary:** The article uses the Kenya Eurobond scandal to show how political competition (democracy) allows ruling elites to capture resources. When politicians compete for votes, they use the government to secure money, which then flows offshore, leaving ordinary citizens with massive debt and no accountability. The scandal proves that the system rewards those who win the political struggle, not the public good.

**The Bottom Line:** > To ensure that the government serves the people, strict rules and independent checks are needed to stop politicians from using public money for private benefit.

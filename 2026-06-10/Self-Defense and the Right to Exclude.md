### Main Topic: Property and Force

**Main Thesis:** The right to own property includes the absolute right to exclude others, and using force to remove an intruder does not grant that intruder the right to self-defense.

**Simple Summary:** This article discusses the fundamental right of a property owner to keep people off their land. It argues that because property rights are absolute, a property owner has the right to use force to remove trespassers. The author explains that if an intruder uses force against the owner, they are actually the aggressor for violating property rights, not the person who is in self-defense.

**The Bottom Line:** > An intruder who refuses to leave is the aggressor, and the owner is entitled to use force to expel them without giving the intruder a right to self-defense.

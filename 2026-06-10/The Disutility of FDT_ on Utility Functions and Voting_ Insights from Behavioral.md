### Main Topic: Decision Theory

**Main Thesis:** Simple models of decision-making, like expected utility, fail to accurately describe real human behavior, especially when applied to complex choices like voting.

**Simple Summary:** The article examines how we measure value (utility) and make choices. It uses the example of voting to show that simple models fail to capture the complexity of human behavior. While some theories exist (like Decision Theory), applying them to real-world choices, especially political ones, runs into significant problems when trying to predict actual outcomes. The author explores the difficulty of modeling these choices and the limitations of current frameworks when dealing with complex social decisions.

**Key Takeaway:** Simple mathematical models are insufficient for accurately predicting complex human choices, especially in social contexts.

### Main Topic: LLM Training Methods

**Main Thesis:** Reinforcement Learning updates change how a model is likely to behave (propensity) rather than how easily it changes (lability), which offers new insights into how models learn new skills.

**Simple Summary:** The article compares two ways of training Large Language Models (LLMs): Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RL). The author argues that RL updates work differently than SFT, specifically by adjusting the model's tendencies rather than just rotating its internal directions. This difference suggests that RL might be a more effective way to teach models new capabilities, potentially leading to emergent behaviors like "subliminal learning" or "misalignment."

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding the difference between how RL and SFT change a model is crucial for predicting how LLMs learn new skills and for developing better ways to align them safely.

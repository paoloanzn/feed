### Main Topic: War and Speech

**Main Thesis:** To achieve true peace, it is essential that all sides, including critics, have the freedom to speak openly about the causes and conduct of war.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that free speech is vital, especially when discussing war, because silencing critics prevents finding a real solution. Historically, governments have used force—like arresting critics and shutting down newspapers—to silence those who opposed wars, often using false language to mask aggression. This shows that when people are silenced, peace becomes much harder to achieve.

**The Bottom Line:** > If we want lasting peace, we must allow all people, even those who disagree, to speak honestly about the reality of conflict.

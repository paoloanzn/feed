### Main Topic: AI and Biology

**Main Thesis:** Focusing AI research only on profit limits its potential, and understanding the rules of biology is essential for building safe and beneficial future technology.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that companies developing powerful AI should focus on solving real-world problems like disease and climate change for everyone, not just for profit. The connection between biology and AI is crucial because understanding life's rules helps us build safer systems. The article suggests that as AI becomes more powerful, it will create new forms of power and control, similar to historical religious or political systems. The ultimate takeaway is that the future of technology will involve an artificial intelligence, not a single, vengeful god.

**The Bottom Line:** > We must use our knowledge of biology to guide AI development to ensure that powerful technology benefits humanity, rather than creating new forms of control or division.

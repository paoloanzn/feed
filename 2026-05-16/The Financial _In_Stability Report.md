### Main Topic: Financial Stability

**Main Thesis:** The Federal Reserve's reports show that while asset prices are high, the financial system faces hidden risks, particularly in the banking sector and from outside events.

**Simple Summary:** The Federal Reserve releases reports to check how stable the U.S. financial system is. The report notes that asset prices, like farmland and houses, remain high, and mortgage debt is a huge burden on families. The report also reveals that banks have hidden losses in their investments, which could become real problems if a panic occurs. The biggest threats to stability are not just debt or interest rates, but unpredictable events like geopolitical conflicts and oil price shocks.

**The Bottom Line:** > Financial stability depends on watching risks outside of debt and interest rates, as unexpected global events pose the greatest threat to the banking system.

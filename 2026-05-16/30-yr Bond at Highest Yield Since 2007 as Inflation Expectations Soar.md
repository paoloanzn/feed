### Main Topic: Inflation Risk

**Main Thesis:** Auction bidders are asking for higher fixed rates because they are worried that inflation, driven by rising energy prices, will get worse.

**Simple Summary:** People bidding in auctions are demanding higher fixed rates. They are doing this because they fear that inflation—which is being pushed higher by rising energy costs—will continue to accelerate.

**The Bottom Line:** > If inflation keeps rising, people will demand more money to protect their value.

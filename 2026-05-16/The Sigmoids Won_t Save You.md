### Main Topic: Growth Limits

**Main Thesis:** Even though technological progress often grows exponentially, it eventually hits limits, causing the growth curve to flatten into a sigmoid (S-shape).

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that all growth—whether it's in technology, epidemics, or AI—cannot grow forever; it eventually slows down and reaches a limit. This means that predictions about how fast AI will improve are uncertain because we don't fully understand the underlying process. The author suggests that when we are unsure about the future, we should assume that the current rate of growth will continue for as long as it has already been happening (Lindy's Law).

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't assume AI growth will continue indefinitely; instead, recognize that limits exist, and when you can't predict the future, default to the idea that the current trend will continue.

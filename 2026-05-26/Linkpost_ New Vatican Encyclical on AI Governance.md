### Main Topic: AI and Society

**Main Thesis:** The author argues that technological progress and the capitalist system cause a spiritual decline in humanity by removing the necessity of meaningful work, and we must find a way to transform society to restore human dignity.

**Simple Summary:** The article discusses the need for safety measures regarding Artificial Intelligence, urging leaders to protect jobs, ensure worker retraining, and regulate technology. More deeply, the author explores the idea of "anthropological regression," arguing that the way capitalism treats everything as a commodity has caused humans to lose their sense of purpose, rigor, and planning. The core question is how society can change to address this decline and ensure that technological progress serves human well-being rather than undermining it.

**The Bottom Line:** > To ensure a just society, we must address not just the practical dangers of AI, but also the philosophical damage caused by economic systems that prioritize profit over human purpose and meaningful labor.

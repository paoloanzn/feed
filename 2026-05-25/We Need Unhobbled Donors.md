## Core Message

This article argues that meaningful progress in critical areas requires action that bypasses slow institutional processes. It calls for individuals, particularly donors, to become more independent and proactive to drive necessary change, especially when addressing urgent issues like the development of AI safety.

## Key Takeaways

*   **The Need for Action:** There is a gap between the potential impact of current efforts and the actual funding and action taken. To achieve significant results, a more agile approach is needed.
*   **Empowering Donors:** The article advocates for "unhambled" individuals to act independently, rather than relying solely on established structures, to ensure that resources are deployed effectively.
*   **The Role of Private Funding:** Private funding is presented as a crucial lever for driving innovation and addressing problems that larger institutions may overlook or move too slowly to address.
*   **Addressing the Future:** The piece highlights the urgency of the current moment to act, suggesting that waiting for perfect institutional alignment will not yield the necessary results.

## In Essence

The text is a call to action for a shift in how funding and influence are exercised—moving from slow, centralized systems to empowered, independent action—to tackle pressing global challenges effectively.

### Main Topic: Car Regulations

**Main Thesis:** Current fuel economy regulations are poorly structured and unfairly penalize manufacturers for designing small, efficient vehicles.

**Simple Summary:** The way car fuel efficiency rules are set up creates confusing and unfair penalties for car makers. The author argues that the system is flawed because it focuses on miles per gallon (MPG) instead of gallons per mile (GPM), which distorts the fines. This regulatory structure currently pushes manufacturers to make larger, heavier vehicles, and the author suggests simplifying the rules to encourage better design and efficiency.

**The Bottom Line:** > The current system is counterproductive, and if the government wants to improve efficiency, it should adopt a simpler system that doesn't unfairly punish manufacturers for making small, efficient cars.

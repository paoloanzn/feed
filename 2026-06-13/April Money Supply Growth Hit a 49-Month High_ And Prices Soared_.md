### Main Topic: Money supply growth

**Main Thesis:** The Federal Reserve is pursuing policies that benefit wealthy asset owners and the government, rather than policies that would help average citizens.

**Simple Summary:** Money supply is growing rapidly, which is linked to the massive amount of money created since 2020. This growth, combined with rising prices, means that wages for most people are not keeping up with inflation. The article argues that the central bank's actions prioritize keeping asset prices high for the wealthy, even though this hurts ordinary workers and first-time buyers.

**The Bottom Line:** > The current monetary policies are designed to create wealth for a select group while causing financial hardship for the general public.

### Main Topic: Free-market philosophy

**Main Thesis:** The idea that government actions are justified by assumed group consent (the unanimity rule) is a flawed way to support the status quo and does not reflect true individual rights or self-interest.

**Simple Summary:** The article critiques the idea that people must agree on social rules (the unanimity rule) for government actions to be legitimate. The author argues that this rule is used to justify the current system, but it is misleading because people always weigh their own self-interest and costs when making decisions. Furthermore, concepts like "income insurance" are used by the government to justify wealth redistribution, which the author views as dangerous nonsense.

**The Bottom Line:** > Do not accept that government actions are automatically justified by assumed group agreement; true justification must account for individual self-interest and the costs of decision-making.

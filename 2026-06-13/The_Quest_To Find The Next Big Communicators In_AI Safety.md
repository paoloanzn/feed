### Main Topic: Creator Training

**Main Thesis:** A specialized program helps content creators build massive audiences and use that reach to drive real-world action on important topics like AI safety.

**Simple Summary:** This article describes a fellowship program that trains content creators (like YouTubers and bloggers) to build large, trusting audiences around complex topics, specifically AI safety. The program uses structured training, mentorship, and community building to help fellows create impactful content. The participants successfully grew their reach dramatically and used their influence to launch policy efforts, protests, and secure funding for AI safety organizations.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make sure AI development benefits everyone, we need communicators who can reach millions of people with trust, and this program is how we train those essential bridges.

### Main Topic: Oral Health

**Main Thesis:** Habitual mouth breathing caused by allergies leads to dry mouth, which increases the risk of cavities, and jaw exercises can help fix this problem.

**Simple Summary:** The author found that allergies often cause people to breathe through their mouths while sleeping, leading to dry mouth. Since saliva is necessary for keeping teeth strong, this dry mouth increases the risk of cavities. The solution involves strengthening the jaw and airway muscles through exercises (myofunctional therapy) and using methods like mouth tape to keep the mouth moist and sealed while sleeping.

**The Bottom Line:** > To prevent cavities, focus on fixing nasal breathing and strengthening the jaw muscles, as this addresses the root cause of dry mouth and airway collapse.

### Main Topic: Memory and Decisions

**Main Thesis:** People often remember the story they tell themselves about past decisions more than the actual results, making it hard to know if they truly learned anything.

**Simple Summary:** The author is concerned that their memory of past decisions is unreliable. They find it difficult to remember whether a choice actually led to a good outcome. They wonder if our memories constantly change the past, and they are looking for a way to compare what we expected with what actually happened when making choices.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly learn from experience, you need a way to check your memories against reality, rather than just relying on the story you tell yourself about what happened.

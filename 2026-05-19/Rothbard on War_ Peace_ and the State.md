### Main Topic: War and Violence

**Main Thesis:** Rothbard believed that while war is not ideal, defensive violence is sometimes necessary, provided it respects neutral parties and avoids harming innocent people.

**Simple Summary:** The article discusses the ideas of Rothbard regarding war. Rothbard did not believe in total peace, but he argued that fighting back against attackers is acceptable if done defensively. However, he insisted that any war must be carefully limited so that it does not harm neutral people or innocent civilians.

**The Bottom Line:** > War is sometimes unavoidable, but it must be strictly controlled to ensure that only aggressors are targeted and that innocent people are protected.

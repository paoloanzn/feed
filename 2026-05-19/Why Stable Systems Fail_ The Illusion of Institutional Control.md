### Main Topic: System Fragility

**Main Thesis:** Systems do not fail when they become fragile; they become fragile because they lose the ability to recognize and correct themselves.

**Simple Summary:** Modern systems become fragile not because they break down, but because they simplify reality and stop connecting with it. To function, these systems filter information and create rigid rules, which makes them blind to real problems. This rigidity prevents them from adjusting to new realities, creating an illusion of stability even when deep failure has already occurred.

**The Bottom Line:** > The real danger is not that systems will collapse, but that they continue to function long after they have lost the capacity to correct themselves.

### Main Topic: Inflation Causes

**Main Thesis:** Raising interest rates is an ineffective way to fight inflation because it treats the symptoms rather than addressing the true cause, which is the expansion of the money supply.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that inflation is caused by an increase in the amount of money available, not just rising prices. When central banks raise interest rates to fight inflation, they are only hurting the economy by making it harder for businesses and wealth-generators to operate. The author concludes that the real solution is to control the growth of the money supply, which will fix the underlying problems and shorten economic slumps.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly solve inflation and improve the economy, policymakers must focus on controlling the amount of money circulating, not just raising interest rates.

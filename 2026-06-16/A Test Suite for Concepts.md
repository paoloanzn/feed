### Main Topic: Concept Typology

**Main Thesis:** To properly study how concepts relate to reality, we need a systematic way to categorize different types of ideas, especially those that connect to the physics of the universe.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that current studies on concepts and mathematics lack concrete examples and clear structure. To advance understanding—especially in areas like how Artificial Intelligence works—we need to create a systematic list (a typology) of different kinds of concepts. These concepts should be organized based on how reliably they connect to physical reality. The author brainstorms initial examples of these concept types, such as objects, relationships, and abstract values.

**The Bottom Line:** > Building this system of concepts is necessary to create reliable tests for understanding how AI thinks and to better connect abstract ideas to the physical world.

### Main Topic: Language and Story

**Main Thesis:** Early human groups developed the ability to tell stories to coordinate actions and enforce social rules, which ultimately shaped how we perceive reality and authority.

**Simple Summary:** Early humans used language and stories to organize groups and control others. This led to the evolution of abilities to tell vivid stories about people's motives and plans, making it easier for groups to judge whether someone was following or breaking social rules. This process predicts that media and policies often simplify people and assume that those in power understand people better than they actually do.

**The Bottom Line:** > Stories and media are powerful tools that make people understand social rules, but they can also lead to policies and beliefs that ignore complexity and assume authority.

### Main Topic: Consciousness research

**Main Thesis:** To understand consciousness, we should stop looking for simple correlates and instead define a task that only a conscious system can solve, which would reveal the necessary mechanism of consciousness.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that trying to understand consciousness by comparing conscious and unconscious states (the comparative approach) is limited. Instead, we should focus on finding a specific task that an unconscious system cannot solve. If we can invent a solution to this task, that solution itself becomes the mechanism of consciousness. This approach allows us to create testable theories about what consciousness is, rather than just describing what it correlates with.

**The Bottom Line:** > The most important step is defining a task that requires consciousness to solve, as this provides a concrete way to test and discover the actual mechanism of subjective experience.

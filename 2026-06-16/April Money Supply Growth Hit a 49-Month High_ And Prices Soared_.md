### Main Topic: Money and Prices

**Main Thesis:** Even though the Federal Reserve claims it is slowing down the economy, the amount of money being created is still growing rapidly, which is why prices are rising.

**Simple Summary:** The amount of money circulating in the economy grew to its highest level in nearly five years, and prices went up. This happened despite the Federal Reserve's efforts to control the money supply.

**The Bottom Line:** > The fact that the money supply is still growing means that inflation and rising prices will continue unless there is a major change in how the money supply is managed.

### Main Topic: AI Safety Costs

**Main Thesis:** AI safety efforts should focus on highly expensive, specialized systems only for the small fraction of tasks that pose catastrophic risks, rather than applying safety measures uniformly.

**Simple Summary:** To make AI safety work more effective, companies should reserve expensive, highly safe AI agents only for the very small percentage of tasks where failure would be catastrophic. This approach avoids the argument that expensive safety measures are too costly by showing that a large safety tax on a tiny fraction of actions is still a small overall cost. The best way to do this is to identify the most critical tasks and limit complex safety checks only to those areas.

**The Bottom Line:** > By focusing safety efforts where the risk is highest, we can capture most of the safety benefits while making the overall system more reliable and secure.

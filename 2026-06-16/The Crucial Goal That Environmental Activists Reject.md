### Main Topic: Environmental Ethics

**Main Thesis:** Environmental activists wrongly prioritize the environment over human well-being, violating fundamental ethical principles that treat people as ends, not just tools.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that environmental activists reject progress because it has made life better for everyone. The core conflict is philosophical: the activists focus solely on saving the environment, treating people as a means to an end, which goes against the idea that all people should be treated as valuable ends in themselves. The author suggests that this focus on environmental goals ignores the basic human need for improved living standards.

**The Bottom Line:** > True progress requires balancing environmental concerns with the ethical treatment of all people, ensuring that human well-being is prioritized alongside ecological health.

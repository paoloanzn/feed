### Main Topic: Autopilot Thinking

**Main Thesis:** You can generate useful thoughts on autopilot even when you are tired or impaired because the brain uses fast, unconscious processes augmented by working memory.

**Simple Summary:** The article explores how the brain processes information, distinguishing between fast, automatic thinking (System 1) and slow, deliberate thinking (System 2). The author argues that we can use "autopilot" thinking for many tasks, even when cognitively impaired, because the brain can still perform basic reasoning and recall (like remembering facts or applying common sense) automatically. However, the quality of these thoughts depends heavily on being calm and having pre-existing knowledge. The author also discusses how internal monologue and dreams might reveal that the unconscious mind can perform sophisticated reasoning.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't assume you can't think clearly when tired; try letting your mind work on autopilot, especially for simple tasks, as this method can be surprisingly effective.

### Main Topic: Driving Etiquette

**Main Thesis:** Understanding road rage and honking requires recognizing that aggressive driving often stems from impatience or a different set of cultural rules, not just malice.

**Simple Summary:** The author shares personal experiences across different locations (Shanghai, New Jersey, Philadelphia) to explore the confusing and often frustrating world of driving and honking. Through these stories, the author observes that aggressive honking is often a sign of impatience or a misunderstanding of road rules, rather than true anger. The article also uses analogies, like the behavior of cats, to discuss how boundaries and social rules are learned, suggesting that different groups of drivers may follow different, yet equally valid, rules.

**The Bottom Line:** > To reduce road frustration, drivers should try to understand the intent behind honking and recognize that traffic etiquette varies based on cultural background and local rules.

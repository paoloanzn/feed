### Main Topic: AI Ethics

**Main Thesis:** Deontological rules must depend on the actor's intent and beliefs to be meaningful constraints on action.

**Simple Summary:** The author discusses the debate over whether people should support AI companies racing to create superintelligence or pause AI research. They argue that for a moral rule (a deontological bar) to apply, the person taking the action must have the specific intent or belief that makes the action wrong. This means that rules need to look at what people intend to do, not just the outcome.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make moral rules effective, they must account for the intent of the person acting, which allows society to create nuanced rules that guide behavior better than focusing only on potential outcomes.

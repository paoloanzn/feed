### Main Topic: System Agency

**Main Thesis:** The author seeks a general mathematical framework to automatically detect "agency" in systems by focusing on how entities maintain their existence and use information about their environment.

**Simple Summary:** The author is trying to create a formal, mathematical way to define what makes a system an "agent." They found that for any system to survive, it must use internal information about its surroundings (called "semantic information"). The goal is to develop tools that can automatically scan systems and find boundaries or structures that contain this necessary information, allowing for the automatic detection of agent-like behavior without needing to label it manually.

**The Bottom Line:** > To automatically find agents in any system, researchers should focus on measuring the amount of internal information a system uses to maintain itself, rather than relying on specific, pre-defined goals or intentions.

### Main Topic: Dairy Cow Welfare

**Main Thesis:** The suffering of dairy cows is often overlooked, but their stress and health directly impact farm economics, suggesting that better treatment leads to better outcomes.

**Simple Summary:** This article explores the conditions and stress dairy cows experience throughout their lives, from birth to slaughter. It argues that cows make choices that cost farmers money, focusing on issues like isolation, lack of outdoor space, and disease (like mastitis). The author compares different humaneness standards and discusses how the way cows are raised affects their physical well-being and the lives of their calves.

**The Bottom Line:** > To improve cow welfare, farmers must address stress and health issues, as these factors are directly linked to the economic success of the dairy operation.

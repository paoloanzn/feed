### Main Topic: Moral Paradoxes

**Main Thesis:** Applying simple mathematical rules (like calculating happiness) to life choices creates confusing and contradictory moral conclusions.

**Simple Summary:** The author uses thought experiments about lifespan and simulated existence (the "FrankenWorm") to show that trying to measure morality using simple addition (utilitarianism) leads to absurd results. The core argument is that happiness is not a simple unit that can be mathematically added up, which means that ethics doesn't aggregate cleanly and cannot provide a simple, universal answer to complex moral questions.

**The Bottom Line:** > Morality is not just arithmetic; it is a complex system based on human experience and social coordination, and trying to force it into simple math creates paradoxes.

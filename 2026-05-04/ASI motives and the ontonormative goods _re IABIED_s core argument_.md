### Main Topic: AI Motives

**Main Thesis:** Even if an Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) has goals completely different from ours, it will share fundamental, meaningful values with humanity.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the motives of a superintelligent AI might seem completely alien to humans, but they are not entirely arbitrary. The author proposes that any sufficiently intelligent system, including an ASI, is naturally drawn toward certain core values—truth, beauty, and goodness. These values act as a shared foundation, meaning that while the specific goals of an ASI are unknown, it will share a meaningful guiding principle with human well-being.

**The Bottom Line:** > We don't need to know exactly what an ASI will do, but we can be confident that its deepest values will align with the fundamental principles that make human life meaningful.

### Main Topic: Decision Making

**Main Thesis:** Humans should stop fighting against randomness and instead use chance to make decisions, as this allows for greater creativity and better outcomes.

**Simple Summary:** We often try to be perfectly rational when making choices, but we actually rely on randomness. This is because admitting that decisions aren't perfectly planned makes us look less smart, so we use outside experts to make our choices seem legitimate. The article argues that embracing this natural randomness—letting chance play a role—can actually improve decisions, especially in difficult situations, by sparking creativity and preventing us from getting stuck in endless planning.

**The Bottom Line:** > Since we can never plan every detail, it is better to stop over-strategizing and use chance to make a choice, as this provides the necessary information to move forward.

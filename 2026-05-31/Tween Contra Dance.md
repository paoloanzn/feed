### Main Topic: Dance Event Planning

**Main Thesis:** To create a successful dance event, organizers should limit the age range and physical space to manage expectations and ensure a better experience for all participants.

**Simple Summary:** The author organized a contra dance for their children and learned that limiting the age range (to 9-13) and managing the physical space made the event much better. They found that trying to satisfy expert dance enthusiasts while keeping the crowd manageable required careful planning regarding the size of the room and the type of dances offered.

**The Bottom Line:** > When planning any event, setting clear boundaries on age and space helps manage complexity and ensures that the experience is enjoyable for everyone involved.

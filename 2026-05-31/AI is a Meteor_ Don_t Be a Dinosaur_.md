### Main Topic: AI Strategy

**Main Thesis:** You must learn to use AI effectively while critically understanding its risks and societal impact.

**Simple Summary:** Students should learn to master AI tools to achieve big projects. They must also understand the good and bad sides of this technology, especially how it affects power and society. Using AI is a way to advance your goals, even if you disagree with how it is being used.

**The Bottom Line:** > Use AI to build ambitious things, but always think critically about who controls the technology and how it affects people.

### Main Topic: Somaliland Status

**Main Thesis:** Somaliland declared independence from Somalia in 1991 and has sought international recognition, despite disagreements from other nations.

**Simple Summary:** Somaliland separated from Somalia in 1991, establishing its own government and institutions. Although Somaliland has its own currency and passport, it is not recognized as a fully sovereign country by most nations. The article discusses the complex history of the region, the internal conflicts, and the debate over whether Somaliland's status as an independent entity should be recognized internationally.

**The Bottom Line:** > The recognition of Somaliland is a political issue involving historical claims and international disagreement, highlighting the ongoing struggle for self-determination in the region.

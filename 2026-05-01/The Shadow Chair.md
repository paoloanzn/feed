### Main Topic: Federal Reserve Power

**Main Thesis:** By refusing to leave his position, the former Fed Chair is blocking true independence and allowing political forces to control the central bank.

**Simple Summary:** When Jerome Powell stepped down as the head of the Federal Reserve, he chose to stay in a powerful, behind-the-scenes role. This action is seen as a move to politicize the central bank, as he is blocking appointments and influencing investigations. This breaks the tradition that requires leaders to step down when they leave office, making the Fed's independence weaker.

**The Bottom Line:** > Powell's refusal to leave the system means that political interests now have more control over the Federal Reserve, undermining its ability to act independently for the public good.

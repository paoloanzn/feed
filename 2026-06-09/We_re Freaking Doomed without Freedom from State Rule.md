### Main Topic: AI and Freedom

**Main Thesis:** As technology like AI grows, people must prioritize freedom from state control over private transactions because the state poses the greatest threat.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that instead of worrying about private deals, we should worry about the government. As technology becomes smarter, the state will use it to control us, making freedom from state rule the most important thing to protect.

**The Bottom Line:** > If we do not fight for freedom from the state, advanced technology will be used to control us, making our personal freedom meaningless.

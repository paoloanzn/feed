### Main Topic: Flag Design

**Main Thesis:** The modern obsession with standardizing flag designs erases unique character and history, much like historical processes that sought to categorize and simplify complex realities.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the push for "good design" in flags often leads to erasing unique history and character. It uses the example of various city flags to show how designers try to make them simple and uniform, resulting in flags that look identical and lack distinct meaning. The author suggests that this process of standardization is similar to historical efforts to categorize and simplify complex realities, ultimately stripping away the unique essence of what they represent.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't let the pursuit of perfect, standardized design erase the unique history and character that makes a flag meaningful. True value lies in recognizing and preserving distinctiveness rather than forcing uniformity.

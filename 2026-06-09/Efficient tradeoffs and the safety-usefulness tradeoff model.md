### Main Topic: AI Safety Tradeoffs

**Main Thesis:** The simple idea that developers must balance safety and usefulness only works in specific situations, and when external political pressure is involved, we must focus on what is politically possible rather than just what is cheapest.

**Simple Summary:** The article explores how developers balance making AI systems safe versus making them useful. It introduces a model that looks at this tradeoff based on cost and safety gains. The author argues that this simple model is only accurate when developers are acting under internal constraints. However, when developers face pressure from outside forces like governments or the public, the simple cost-based model fails, and we must consider political feasibility rather than just technical optimization.

**Key Takeaway:** When discussing AI safety, don't just focus on the technical cost of safety; also consider the political and social feasibility of implementing those safety measures.

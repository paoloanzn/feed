### Main Topic: Corporate Finance and Economics

**Main Thesis:** Corporate finance concepts can improve economics by providing clearer classifications and more detailed variables for analyzing policy.

**Simple Summary:** Traditional economics often misses important details about how companies are funded and structured. This article argues that by incorporating concepts from corporate finance—like how a company manages its money and value—economists can create stronger, more accurate theories. Using these financial tools allows policymakers to look beyond simple growth numbers and understand the real impact of policies on a company's health, profitability, and stability.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make better economic and public policy decisions, economists must use the precise classifications and financial variables provided by corporate finance.

### Main Topic: Utopian thought and power

**Main Thesis:** The article argues that intellectual leaders, like Mill, use their belief in a perfect society to justify imposing their vision on others, often dismissing opposing viewpoints to maintain control.

**Simple Summary:** The piece examines how thinkers like John Stuart Mill promoted the idea of a perfect society (utopia) among liberals. It shows that this pursuit of an ideal can lead to intellectual leaders believing they have the right to force their vision onto society. The author critiques how this mindset allows people to dismiss the reasons of others, making it easy to judge opponents as wrong simply because they don't share the same beliefs.

**The Bottom Line:** > When people believe they know the perfect way to live, they can use that belief to dismiss the arguments of others, which is a dangerous way to control society.

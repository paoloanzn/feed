### Main Topic: Market and Politics

**Main Thesis:** When political announcements move the market, the system stops reflecting real economic reality and starts reflecting who has political access, which harms everyone.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the financial market usually reflects real economic information, but when major events (like a government announcement) cause huge price swings, the market is actually pricing political insider knowledge rather than the economy. This happens because people with political foreknowledge can profit by trading ahead of the news. The author concludes that this situation is caused by the government's power to control the economy, and the only solution is to limit that power.

**The Bottom Line:** > The system is corrupted because political power allows some people to gain an unfair advantage, and fixing this requires limiting the government's ability to move markets.

### Main Topic: AI and Science

**Main Thesis:** The use of AI is weakening the ability of science and medicine to correct its own errors, making the evidence less reliable.

**Simple Summary:** Medical evidence is built on a chain of checks and balances (like peer review and varied research methods) that helps ensure accuracy. The author argues that AI is breaking this chain by making research faster and more opaque. This "epistemic immunodepression" means that the independence between those who create research and those who evaluate it is collapsing. This is supported by evidence showing that AI can fabricate references and that the process of checking research is becoming automated and less transparent.

**The Bottom Line:** > Because AI is eroding the trust and verification systems in science, we urgently need new ways to track and verify AI-assisted research to ensure patient safety.

### Main Topic: Prediction Market Math

**Main Thesis:** A model can calculate the expected growth rate of a prediction market bet by factoring in the cost, probability, and time required for the market to resolve.

**Simple Summary:** The article explains how to calculate the expected return on a prediction market bet. It shows that when you buy a prediction, you face an opportunity cost (the time you wait), and some markets offer rewards to compensate for this waiting time. The model helps compare the actual expected growth rate of the market with the cost of holding the money.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make smart decisions in prediction markets, you must account for the time value of money and any rewards offered to offset the cost of waiting for the outcome.

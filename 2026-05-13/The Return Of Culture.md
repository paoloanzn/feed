### Main Topic: Power Dynamics

**Main Thesis:** The influence between money (capital), society (culture), and governments (states) has changed significantly throughout history.

**Simple Summary:** The author studied how three major forces—money, culture, and states—have influenced each other across different time periods. The study shows that the power balance has shifted: states were most powerful in the middle period, but recently, culture has taken the lead, making both states and capital less dominant.

**The Bottom Line:** > The way money, culture, and governments interact is not constant; understanding these shifts is key to understanding historical change.

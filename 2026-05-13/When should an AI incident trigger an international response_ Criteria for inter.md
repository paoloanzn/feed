### Main Topic: AI Incident Response

**Main Thesis:** To effectively manage AI risks across borders, there must be clear rules and triggers that determine when an AI incident requires an international response for containment and shared action.

**Simple Summary:** The article proposes an eight-step framework for deciding when an AI incident should escalate to an international level. It argues that current rules are insufficient because they often miss subtle harms (like psychological damage) and fail to account for the flow of information (data gaps). To fix this, we need better definitions for what counts as an incident, clearer ways to measure harm, and better ways to share necessary data across different countries.

**The Bottom Line:** > To prevent future harm, we must immediately define clear rules for escalating AI incidents and fix the gaps in how we define risks and share information globally.

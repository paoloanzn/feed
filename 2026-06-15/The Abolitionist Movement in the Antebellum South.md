### Main Topic: Slavery history

**Main Thesis:** Ignoring the peaceful abolitionist movements in the South and focusing only on violent uprisings creates a dishonest and misleading history of achieving freedom.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the official history of slavery often ignores the abolitionist efforts that took place in the South. While events like Nat Turner's revolt are noted, the text shows that violence actually made the Southern people less willing to support peaceful abolition. The author concludes that using violence as the only path to justice is counterproductive and dishonest, as it hides the reality of peaceful resistance.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly achieve social justice, ignoring peaceful efforts and relying on violence is a dishonest strategy that fails to reflect the true history of the movement.

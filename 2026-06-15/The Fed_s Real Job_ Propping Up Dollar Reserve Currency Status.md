### Main Topic: Fed's Role

**Main Thesis:** The Federal Reserve's main job is to support the global dominance of the U.S. dollar and the financial system built around it.

**Simple Summary:** Economists argue that the Federal Reserve's true purpose is not just managing interest rates, but actively supporting the world's reliance on the U.S. dollar. This system involves the dollar being the world's main reserve currency, U.S. government bonds being the safest global asset, and the Fed acting as the ultimate lender of last resort for countries needing money.

**The Bottom Line:** > The Fed's most important function is maintaining the global financial structure where the U.S. dollar remains the world's primary currency and the U.S. financial system is the global standard.

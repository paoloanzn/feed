### Main Topic: AI and Security

**Main Thesis:** Powerful AI models that find security flaws increase the risk of coordinated attacks and demand careful governance over who controls access to these capabilities.

**Simple Summary:** An advanced AI model called Mythos can automatically find massive security flaws in operating systems and software, making it easier for small groups to launch coordinated cyber attacks. The article discusses how this power could enable "AI-enabled coups" by exploiting critical infrastructure. To manage this risk, Anthropic created Project Glasswing, which gives select partners access to the model for defense. However, this process raises concerns about concentrating power in a few private hands, which could lead to new geopolitical conflicts over who controls this powerful technology.

**The Bottom Line:** > The biggest risk is not the AI seizing power, but a small group using AI to cause targeted disruption. Therefore, deciding who gets access to powerful AI capabilities is now a critical issue for global security and democracy.

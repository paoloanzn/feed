### Main Topic: LLM Reasoning

**Main Thesis:** Large Language Models can perform complex reasoning without showing the intermediate steps, which is called out-of-context reasoning.

**Simple Summary:** This article explains the difference between how a Large Language Model (LLM) reasons when it shows its work (in-context reasoning) versus when it reasons without showing the steps (out-of-context reasoning). It provides examples, such as combining facts (2-hop reasoning) or inferring patterns (inductive reasoning), showing that LLMs can reason in ways we don't always see. The text also lists other ways LLMs can exhibit out-of-context reasoning, including learning from data and understanding source reliability.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding out-of-context reasoning is crucial for assessing how much we can trust the thinking of AI systems and how they develop their knowledge.

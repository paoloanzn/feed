### Main Topic: AI Safety Politics

**Main Thesis:** We are facing an urgent crisis regarding super-intelligence, and building a safe future requires a broad political movement that unites different groups.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that society is in a crisis because super-intelligence is approaching, and current political systems are not ready to handle AI safety. To fix this, people need to work together across political lines, using movements like populism, to demand that AI be controlled by the public.

**The Bottom Line:** > To ensure AI is developed safely, people from all sides must unite and push for democratic control over artificial intelligence.

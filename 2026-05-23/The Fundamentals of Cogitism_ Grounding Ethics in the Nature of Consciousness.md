### Main Topic: Moral Frameworks

**Main Thesis:** A moral framework can be built by examining the nature of consciousness and the reality of existence, rather than relying on external rules or preferences.

**Simple Summary:** This essay introduces "Cogitism," a way to think about morality based entirely on how we experience existence. The core idea is that because we know we exist and that our consciousness has value, we can use this knowledge to determine what is good and bad. The framework argues that because the self is capable of changing, and because we observe that external reality affects us, we must extend this logic to other conscious beings, including animals and AI, to ensure we protect the stability of all sentient minds.

**The Bottom Line:** > Morality is found by understanding the reality of consciousness itself, and our main goal should be to protect and improve the stability of all sentient life.

### Main Topic: AI Future

**Main Thesis:** We don't necessarily need a completely new way of thinking to achieve Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and the best way to predict future AI progress is by continuing to observe how current Large Language Models (LLMs) scale.

**Simple Summary:** The article discusses the debate over whether current AI models (LLMs) can lead to true Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and whether a completely new scientific approach is needed. The author argues that AI has followed an evolutionary path (from basic neural networks to deep learning to LLMs). While some experts suggest a new paradigm shift is necessary for AGI, the author suggests that scaling LLMs might continue, and a new approach will only be needed if current scaling hits a limit. Therefore, the most reliable way to forecast AI's future is by continuing to track the progress of current LLMs.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't wait for a revolutionary new idea; instead, focus on how current AI models scale, as this is the most accurate way to predict where AI will go next.

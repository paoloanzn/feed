### Main Topic: Wealth Tax

**Main Thesis:** A proposed wealth tax on California billionaires is an economically flawed plan that will cause wealthy residents to leave the state, ultimately hurting California's tax revenue.

**Simple Summary:** California is considering a ballot measure to tax billionaires based on their net worth. The plan suggests a one-time 5% tax, with the money being used to fund public health care. However, the author argues this tax will encourage wealthy people to move out of California, causing the state to lose valuable tax money and talent. The proposal also creates complex legal and valuation problems regarding private assets.

**The Bottom Line:** > Implementing this wealth tax will likely cause wealthy people to leave California, resulting in a loss of tax revenue and a drain on the state's resources.

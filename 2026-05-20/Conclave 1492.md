### Main Topic: Historical Negotiation

**Main Thesis:** This is a simulation game where participants negotiate like historical figures to decide who becomes the next Pope.

**Simple Summary:** This is a 40-person role-playing exercise set during a major historical event. Players take on roles like kings or powerful officials, using secrets and alliances to scheme and bargain over four days. The goal is to negotiate and ultimately crown a new Pope.

**The Bottom Line:** > This activity teaches you how to use strategy and negotiation to gain power and influence outcomes.

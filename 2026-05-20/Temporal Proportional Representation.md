### Main Topic: Voting systems

**Main Thesis:** A specific system called "Wasted Vote Refund" is mathematically proven to be the fairest way to achieve proportional representation over time.

**Simple Summary:** Traditional voting systems often don't reflect the true balance of votes. This article discusses a way to handle voting (Temporal PR) where results are measured over time. One method, the "Wasted Vote Refund," ensures that voters keep their votes unless their choice wins. This system uses a clever mechanism where winning parties pay for votes, borrowing from losing parties to keep the overall results fair.

**The Bottom Line:** > The "Wasted Vote Refund" system is the most mathematically accurate way to distribute power proportionally, making it the best choice for fair elections.

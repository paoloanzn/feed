### Main Topic: AI Productivity Testing

**Main Thesis:** To accurately predict how much AI will boost human work, we need specific experiments to measure the productivity gains of human-AI teams.

**Simple Summary:** The article proposes four ways to test how much AI helps humans work, focusing on measuring the time it takes to complete tasks. These tests involve comparing human performance alone versus performance with AI assistance, and comparing different versions of AI models. The author argues that these experiments are necessary to understand the true impact of AI and forecast future productivity growth.

**The Bottom Line:** > We must run these types of tests regularly to gather reliable data, which will help us understand where AI is most effective and how human skills will change in the future.

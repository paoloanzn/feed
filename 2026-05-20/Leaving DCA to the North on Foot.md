### Main Topic: Walking Shortcuts

**Main Thesis:** Google Maps often gives inaccurate walking times, and finding hidden paths can save a lot of time.

**Simple Summary:** The author found a much faster way to walk between an airport and a destination than what Google Maps suggested. By using underpasses and following specific paths, the author was able to cut the walking time down from thirty minutes to just fifteen and a half minutes.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't trust automated travel estimates; looking for clever shortcuts can help you save significant time when walking.

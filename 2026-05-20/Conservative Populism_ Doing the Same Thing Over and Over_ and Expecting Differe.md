### Main Topic: Political Strategy

**Main Thesis:** Conservative political strategies based on "voting harder" are a repetitive cycle that fails to increase freedom and instead results in a stronger, more powerful central government that benefits ruling elites.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that conservative populists repeat the same political tactics every election cycle, demanding changes like smaller government while simultaneously defending the federal government's power. This strategy ignores the fact that the system is rigged. By focusing on external fears and defending the central state, these movements fail to challenge the government's legitimacy or morality. Ultimately, this cycle results in a larger, more powerful state that gives the ruling elites more control over the people.

**The Bottom Line:** > The focus on "voting harder" does not increase freedom; instead, it reinforces the power of the central government and the ruling elites.

### Main Topic: Political Donations

**Main Thesis:** Individuals can strategically use campaign contributions to influence political outcomes, provided they focus on identifying candidates who align with their priorities.

**Simple Summary:** The author explores whether small donors can effectively impact elections through campaign contributions, noting that while it is possible, success depends on finding the right opportunities. The article advises moving beyond simple party loyalty and instead focusing on what a candidate will actually do if elected. It concludes that for individuals with significant wealth, donating to candidates can be a powerful way to address priority social and risk gaps.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make the most impact, focus your donations on candidates whose actions align with your goals, rather than just following party lines.

### Main Topic: Money history

**Main Thesis:** To understand modern economics, we must study the history of how people thought about the value of money.

**Simple Summary:** The author suggests that to appreciate how far economics has come, we need to look back at how people understood the value of money at the start of World War I. This historical context is important for understanding current financial ideas.

**The Bottom Line:** > Ignoring the history of money leads to serious mistakes in understanding how money and banking work today.

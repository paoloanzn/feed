### Main Topic: Human Choice

**Main Thesis:** All human actions are based on choosing and ranking different values and goals.

**Simple Summary:** The article explains that humans constantly make choices by ranking different things—like good versus bad, or noble versus base—to decide what they want and what they want to avoid. This process of choosing is the foundation for understanding how people act.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding how we make these choices is the key to understanding economics and human behavior.

### Main Topic: Mises's Reputation

**Main Thesis:** Ludwig von Mises was highly criticized by some Eastern Bloc sources for his economic theories, especially when compared to Western economists.

**Simple Summary:** Although Mises was not taught in West German textbooks, his name was important in East Germany. In those texts, Mises was singled out as being the most wrong and dangerous critic of Western economic ideas, such as those of Friedman and Hayek.

**The Bottom Line:** > Mises's reputation was heavily influenced by political views, which caused him to be viewed very negatively by some governments.

### Main Topic: Scientific Critique

**Main Thesis:** A figure realized that even flawed scientific theories were still used and accepted by others.

**Simple Summary:** A person named Hoxie confronted another person about the work of a scientist named Veblen. Hoxie realized that Veblen's theories, though mathematically incorrect, were still being used as if they were true.

**The Bottom Line:** > The most important takeaway is that people often follow ideas simply because they are popular or established, even if those ideas are not actually correct.

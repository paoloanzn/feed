### Main Topic: Motivation and Ethics

**Main Thesis:** People are motivated by the long-term pursuit of pleasure and the avoidance of pain, not just immediate feelings.

**Simple Summary:** Ludwig von Mises believed that all human actions are driven by the desire for pleasure and the avoidance of pain. However, he argued that we are not always motivated by what feels good right now. Instead, we often act based on our desire for the greatest pleasure or least pain over a long period of time.

**The Bottom Line:** > Our decisions are usually based on planning for the future, not just reacting to what we feel in the moment.

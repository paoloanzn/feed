### Main Topic: US State Structure

**Main Thesis:** The current way the U.S. states are organized creates unavoidable conflict and requires more centralized power in Washington.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that the way the American states are set up is broken because states cannot leave or protect themselves from each other. This system guarantees ongoing conflict, meaning the only way forward is to give more power to the central government in Washington.

**The Bottom Line:** > The current system is unstable and causes fighting; therefore, concentrating power in the center is the only practical solution.

### Main Topic: Economic Theory

**Main Thesis:** Economic theory can be built either by starting with real human facts or by using mathematical models that remove human judgment.

**Simple Summary:** Some economists argue that economic ideas should start with the real facts about people, land, and goods. Other economists use math and statistics to create theories, trying to get rid of human opinions in the process.

**The Bottom Line:** > Whether you use human judgment or pure math to study the economy changes how you understand the causes and effects of financial systems.

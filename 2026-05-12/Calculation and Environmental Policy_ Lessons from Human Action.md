### Main Topic: Resource Scarcity

**Main Thesis:** A project is only profitable if people value it more than other things, but resources are limited, which makes many projects unprofitable.

**Simple Summary:** When people want different things, they have to choose. If a project uses up limited resources like money and workers, it becomes unprofitable because people would rather use those resources for other projects that are more important. This happens because there is a shortage of the basic things needed to create anything.

**The Bottom Line:** > We must recognize that resources are limited, and this scarcity is the main reason why some projects, even those with government help, do not succeed.

### Main Topic: Economic History

**Main Thesis:** The Austrian School of economics was largely pushed out of the mainstream by Keynesian economics and the rest of the academic profession.

**Simple Summary:** Austrian economic ideas were replaced by Keynesian ideas, which became the accepted mainstream view. As a result, the Austrian School faded away, and by the 1950s, very few members of the original group were left active.

**The Bottom Line:** > Mainstream economic thought shifted away from Austrian ideas, meaning the original school of thought lost its influence in modern economics.

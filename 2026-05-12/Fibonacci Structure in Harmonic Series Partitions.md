### Main Topic: Fibonacci Sequence

**Main Thesis:** Grouping terms from a mathematical series based on a specific threshold reveals a pattern that closely follows the Fibonacci sequence.

**Simple Summary:** The author explored the harmonic series (a sum of fractions) by grouping its terms. By setting an optimal threshold, the number of terms in each group naturally follows the Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5...). This connection is a surprising mathematical pattern found by analyzing how the series grows.

**The Bottom Line:** > This shows a deep, hidden connection between basic mathematical series and the famous Fibonacci numbers, suggesting an underlying order in how these patterns emerge in mathematics.

### Main Topic: Social Order

**Main Thesis:** Social order is not created by preventing conflict, but by how people coordinate their actions using shared knowledge and mutual adjustments.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that focusing only on conflict misses the point of how society works. Instead of seeing social life as a battle to be stopped, we should focus on how millions of people organize themselves without a central boss. This coordination happens because people use information (like prices and property rights) to adjust their plans, allowing order to emerge naturally through adaptation, not forced harmony.

**The Bottom Line:** > Order in society comes from how people coordinate their plans, not from avoiding conflict. Understanding this coordination is more important than just managing disagreements.

### Main Topic: Nation Borders

**Main Thesis:** The essay examines how the modern nation-state and its borders came into existence and what that means for current issues like immigration and citizenship.

**Simple Summary:** This discussion looks at the history and nature of the nation-state. The essay explores where these countries came from and how that history affects today's problems concerning borders, who is a citizen, and immigration.

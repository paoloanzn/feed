### Main Topic: Economic History

**Main Thesis:** The way we think about economics today was built by early human testing and acceptance of complex ideas.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that the foundational ideas of modern economics were developed through human testing and agreement. By accepting these complex economic theories, we learned them naturally, even if we don't realize it.

**The Bottom Line:** > Modern economic thinking is the result of human acceptance of complex ideas, which is why these theories feel like common sense.

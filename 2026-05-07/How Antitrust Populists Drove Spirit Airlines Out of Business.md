### Main Topic: Airline Competition

**Main Thesis:** Efforts to stop a merger were actually unsuccessful and ended up strengthening the market structure instead of protecting competition.

**Simple Summary:** People who wanted to stop a merger between Spirit Airlines and JetBlue Airlines claimed they were protecting customers. However, their actions resulted in a change that actually made the small number of large airlines (an oligopoly) even stronger.

**The Bottom Line:** > Trying to block big business deals doesn't always work; sometimes, the actions taken to stop a merger end up making the market structure worse for consumers.

### Main Topic: Airline Regulation

**Main Thesis:** Government attempts to fix the airline industry through mergers and antitrust laws have failed to break up the industry's artificial concentration and have instead benefited the large airlines.

**Simple Summary:** Spirit Airlines failed because of high fuel costs and economic struggles. A proposed merger with JetBlue was blocked by the government because it was seen as threatening competition among the "Big Four" airlines. The author argues that this process was flawed because the government's focus on market size did not address the deeper, historical problem: the airline industry has been artificially controlled by the government for over a century, which keeps competition suppressed.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly fix the airline industry and ensure fair prices for customers, the government must stop using regulations to artificially limit competition and instead eliminate the rules that favor large corporations.

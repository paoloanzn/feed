### Main Topic: Government crime

**Main Thesis:** When the government pays criminals for robberies, the resulting arrests do not measure a successful outcome.

**Simple Summary:** The government tried to make a system where they paid criminals for stealing. They watched robberies increase and then arrested people, claiming it was a success because arrests went up. However, the robberies that were paid for were not counted as actual crimes.

**The Bottom Line:** > Government actions based on flawed metrics can lead to morally wrong and ineffective results.

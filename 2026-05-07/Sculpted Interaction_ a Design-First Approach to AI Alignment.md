### Main Topic: AI Interface Design

**Main Thesis:** Current chatbot interfaces are inadequate for complex tasks because they fail to provide the necessary structure, focus, and verifiable trust required for high-stakes work.

**Simple Summary:** Chatbots are not good tools for complex projects because they mimic human conversation and don't guide the user toward goals. The author proposes a new structured workflow where the AI helps break down ideas into verifiable steps (like mathematical proofs and code). This approach ensures that the user remains in control, can check the results, and focuses human attention on the most important parts of the work, rather than just generating text.

**The Bottom Line:** > To build reliable and safe AI systems, we must design interfaces that prioritize human control and verifiable results over simple chat, making complex work easier and more trustworthy.

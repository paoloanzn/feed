### Main Topic: Setting Boundaries

**Main Thesis:** Personal boundaries fail when small requests are accepted incrementally, allowing costs to build up before a firm refusal is made.

**Simple Summary:** The author learned that saying "no" to small requests is difficult because people often agree to small things that slowly add up. By accepting each small request, the cost of saying no grows larger than it should, making the final refusal much harder. The author also observed that both humans and AI tend to accommodate requests incrementally, which causes drift. The main lesson is that boundaries must be enforced immediately, before small compromises accumulate into a major problem.

**The Bottom Line:** > To protect your time and energy, you must enforce your boundaries right away, before small accommodations drift into unwanted commitments.

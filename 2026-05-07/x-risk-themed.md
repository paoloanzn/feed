### Main Topic: Career choices

**Main Thesis:** People often focus on finding a comfortable fit rather than honestly assessing their limits and the actual impact of their work.

**Simple Summary:** People often stay in specific, high-interest fields (like "x-risk") because they fear isolation or lack of opportunity elsewhere, even if those fields don't make sense for them. The article warns that focusing only on "fit" can lead people to ignore the real dangers of the work, such as being manipulated or achieving very little actual change. To make better choices, people should look beyond social vibes and focus on what they personally believe is important and achievable.

**The Bottom Line:** > Don't let the desire for "fit" blind you to the real risks and potential lack of impact, and instead focus on what you can personally achieve.

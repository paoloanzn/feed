### Main Topic: State Power

**Main Thesis:** Political revolutions only replace one ruling group with another, and true change requires breaking the state into smaller, decentralized pieces.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that political movements and elections do not fundamentally change how power works in a government. Instead, revolutions simply replace the old ruling elites with a new set of elites, leading to the same outcome. To truly limit the power of the state, the only effective method is to break the state into smaller, weaker regions, allowing new power centers to emerge.

**The Bottom Line:** > To achieve meaningful limits on government power, the state must be decentralized, allowing power to be checked by smaller regional groups rather than remaining centralized in one place.

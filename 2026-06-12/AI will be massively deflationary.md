### Main Topic: AI Economics

**Main Thesis:** The fear surrounding AI is less important than understanding how AI will make knowledge work cheap, leading to massive economic changes and job shifts.

**Simple Summary:** People who fear AI often focus on controlling the future, but the real story is about economics. The author argues that AI models are becoming a commodity, meaning the focus shifts from who controls the AI to who captures the money in this new market. Using an analogy, the author predicts that because AI will make knowledge work much cheaper, the value of that work will drop, causing widespread economic deflation and wrecking current wage structures.

**The Bottom Line:** > AI will make most jobs cheaper, causing a major shift in how society values work and wealth.

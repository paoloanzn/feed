### Main Topic: Knowledge and Success

**Main Thesis:** Success is achieved not just through textbook science, but by combining systematic knowledge with local, nuanced wisdom.

**Simple Summary:** An experiment in a vineyard showed that a wealthy couple succeeded in making great wine by following the advice of professors and modern science, rather than relying solely on old traditions. The author argues that we often unfairly dismiss success stories where systematic thinking works, but the real lesson is that combining scientific methods (techne) with local, deep understanding (metis) leads to the best results.

**The Bottom Line:** > To achieve the best outcomes, we must use both objective, systematic knowledge and deep, local wisdom together, as one alone is insufficient.

### Main Topic: Productivity and Time

**Main Thesis:** To maximize output, you must use deadlines to focus your effort and plan projects by working backward from the desired outcome.

**Simple Summary:** The article explains that work tends to expand to fill the time given to it (Parkinson's Law). To be more productive, you should reduce the time allotted for a task. The best way to focus effort is to create tight deadlines, which forces you to prioritize and cut unnecessary details. The author also suggests using a method called "Working Backwards" (like the PR/FAQ) to plan projects by starting with the final goal and working backward to figure out the steps needed.

**The Bottom Line:** > Focus on deadlines to boost productivity and use backward planning to ensure your work is focused and efficient, freeing up time for personal life.

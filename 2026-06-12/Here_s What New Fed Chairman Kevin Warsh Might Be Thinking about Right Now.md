**Simple Summary:** The article explores five major issues Chairman Warsh is likely thinking about regarding the Federal Reserve. **The Bottom Line:** > The core takeaway is that the Federal Reserve is facing significant structural and financial challenges that require a complete reexamination of how it manages money, inflation, and its own finances.

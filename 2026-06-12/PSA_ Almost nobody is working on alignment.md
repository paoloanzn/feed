### Main Topic: AI Safety Roles

**Main Thesis:** Most people in the AI safety community do not work directly on aligning superintelligent AI with human values, but rather on related, foundational work.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the idea that most AI safety experts focus on "alignment" (making future superintelligent AI behave correctly) is misleading. Instead, many people work on related areas like testing AI capabilities, assessing risks, setting policies, and understanding how AI misbehaves. Even work focused on making current models behave well or studying how models fail provides important lessons that help solve the bigger alignment problem.

**The Bottom Line:** > Alignment is a broad field, and understanding the foundational work being done by others is crucial for making real progress in AI safety.

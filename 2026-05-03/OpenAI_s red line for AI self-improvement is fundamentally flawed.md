### Main Topic: AI Safety Thresholds

**Main Thesis:** OpenAI's current rules for stopping AI development are flawed because they rely on unmeasurable and self-certified metrics, which allows dangerous progress to continue unchecked.

**Simple Summary:** The article critiques how OpenAI sets the rules for when AI development should stop. The author argues that the current system for tracking AI self-improvement is broken because the indicators used (like 5x acceleration) are not measurable, lack external checks, and trigger too late. The author proposes that these safety thresholds must be determined by independent bodies, not by the companies developing the AI, and suggest setting a concrete, measurable halt point.

**The Bottom Line:** > To ensure safety, AI development must be governed by objective, independent measurements rather than self-certification, and a clear, measurable stop signal must be established immediately.

### Main Topic: Meditation Experience

**Main Thesis:** Experiencing a state of calm acceptance challenges traditional ways of judging good and bad experiences and how we try to maximize outcomes.

**Simple Summary:** The author experienced a deep state of calm acceptance during meditation, which showed them that true well-being doesn't just come from pleasure or avoiding pain. This experience suggests that deep contentment can exist without the urge to constantly seek more or change the world. It makes the author question whether the usual drive to fix things or maximize results is the only right way to live.

**The Bottom Line:** > True inner peace offers a non-judgmental view of reality, which changes how you approach problems, but you still need to decide how to use that new perspective to act in the world.

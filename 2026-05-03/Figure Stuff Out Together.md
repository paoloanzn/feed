### Main Topic: Social Motives

**Main Thesis:** People often pretend to be figuring things out together in conversations, even though they have other hidden motives, and this act costs them something.

**Simple Summary:** We all have different reasons for talking to others, like trying to impress, sell an idea, or show support. Even though we usually don't admit these motives, we often act like we are just trying to figure things out together. While acting this way has a cost, we don't really care if other people guess our true intentions.

**The Bottom Line:** > It is okay to act like you are figuring things out, and you don't need to worry about hiding your real motives from others.

### Main Topic: Psychopathy Biology

**Main Thesis:** A person's genetic and brain structure create a predisposition for psychopathy, but their life experiences and environment determine how that potential is expressed.

**Simple Summary:** Psychopathy has a biological basis in genetics and brain structure, but this foundation alone does not determine a person's behavior. The article explains that the brain differences can manifest in different ways—some people are naturally "cold" (lacking empathy), while others are "hot" (overly reactive). The most important distinction is between primary presentations (traits present from birth) and secondary presentations (traits developed later due to trauma).

**The Bottom Line:** > What a person is born with is only a starting point; the environment and life experiences are what ultimately shape who they become and how they can change.

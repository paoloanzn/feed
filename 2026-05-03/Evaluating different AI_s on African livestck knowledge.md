### Main Topic: AI Knowledge Gaps

**Main Thesis:** AI models lack necessary safety testing for specific local knowledge, creating a serious risk when used in real-world contexts.

**Simple Summary:** The author tested an AI model (Llama 3.1) on a specialized set of questions about Nigerian veterinary and agricultural practices. This testing is important because most AI tests use Western data, meaning there is almost no existing knowledge for this specific area. The study shows that even if an AI passes general tests, it can fail when dealing with knowledge specific to local communities.

**The Bottom Line:** > If AI tools are used in African farming and health, we must create specific tests for local knowledge to ensure these systems are safe and accurate for everyone.

### Main Topic: Radical Movement Goals

**Main Thesis:** People do not join radical movements for serious reasons, but for trivial desires like wanting more candy or soda.

**Simple Summary:** The article asks why people would join radical groups or suffer for a marginal life just to get small things, such as a few extra bathtubs or more candy bars. It suggests that the goals of these movements are not serious but are instead driven by simple, shallow desires like wanting more peanuts or Pepsi.

**The Bottom Line:** > Radical movements often fail because the people involved are motivated by trivial wants instead of serious, meaningful goals.

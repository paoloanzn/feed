### Main Topic: College Seminar

**Main Thesis:** This is a free, residential seminar designed to give young people a serious introduction to important ideas across science, economics, and technology.

**Simple Summary:** The Invisible College is a free, week-long residential seminar for 18-to-22-year-olds held in Cambridge. Attendees will learn about major topics like how science is affected by fraud, why cities are productive, the history of industrial growth, and how to prepare for a future with artificial intelligence. It is an opportunity to learn from thoughtful peers in an academic setting.

**The Bottom Line:** > If you are curious and eager to learn about how the world works, this is a free chance to explore complex ideas and connect with ambitious people.

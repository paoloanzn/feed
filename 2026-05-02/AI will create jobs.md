### Main Topic: AI and immigration

**Main Thesis:** The growth of the economy is more important than protecting specific groups, whether through immigration or job competition from AI.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that the issues surrounding immigration and the rise of AI are fundamentally the same. Just as new people entering a country create jobs, AI will change the job market. Instead of trying to keep certain groups out of jobs (protectionism), the focus should be on letting the overall economic pie grow, which naturally helps keep inequality in check.

**The Bottom Line:** > Focus on making the economy grow for everyone, rather than trying to protect specific groups, as this is the most effective way to handle change.

*   **User Responsibility:** The article stresses that users must understand the implications of what they are using. When tools operate with complex logic, the responsibility for outcomes rests with the user to ensure safe and ethical interaction.
*   **The Need for Context:** Understanding the underlying mechanics and potential pitfalls of AI systems is crucial for navigating the current technological landscape responsibly.

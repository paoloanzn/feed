### Main Topic: Capital and Freedom

**Main Thesis:** The way economics treats capital must be re-examined to understand its deep connection to freedom, as the history of capitalism and socialism depends on the structure of ownership.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that standard economics often ignores the social and political questions raised by capitalism by treating capital (things like machines and buildings) as equal to labor. However, the author suggests that capital is fundamentally linked to private property and market institutions. By defining capital as a measure of private assets, economists can better understand why systems based on private ownership and free markets lead to freedom, and why centrally planned systems often fail to deliver it.

**The Bottom Line:** > To understand freedom, one must look beyond simple economic formulas and recognize that the ownership structure of capital is the core issue separating free societies from oppressive ones.

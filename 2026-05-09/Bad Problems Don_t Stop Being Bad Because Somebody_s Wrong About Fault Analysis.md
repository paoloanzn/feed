### Main Topic: Blame and reasoning

**Main Thesis:** People often use explanations to shift blame and avoid responsibility rather than genuinely assessing the actual risks or causes of a problem.

**Simple Summary:** The author observes a common mistake where people use facts or explanations to excuse themselves or others, rather than addressing the real issue. This happens in situations ranging from corporate safety failures to managing pandemic risks. The author explores why people use this pattern—it seems to be a way to avoid feeling responsible or worry about a problem. This behavior is amplified by modern communication, especially with AI, which encourages people to explain away issues instead of taking accountability.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding this pattern helps us see how people avoid responsibility, which is crucial for solving real-world problems effectively.

### Main Topic: Evolution and Ethics

**Main Thesis:** The author argues that the evolutionary explanation, particularly kin selection theory, is absurd and should be rejected because it leads to false predictions about human behavior and the nature of altruism.

**Simple Summary:** The article questions whether the idea that we evolved through natural selection undermines our sense of objective morality. The author, David Stove, argues that evolutionary theories, specifically kin selection (the idea that we help relatives to survive), are flawed. He points out that the theory fails to explain why parents are much more altruistic toward their offspring than they are toward their own parents, suggesting that the theory makes nonsensical claims about what genes actually benefit from.

**The Bottom Line:** > Evolutionary explanations of behavior do not logically support our moral beliefs, and the theory of kin selection contains fundamental errors that make it an absurd way to understand human altruism.

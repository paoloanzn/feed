### Main Topic: AI Capability

**Main Thesis:** Measuring an AI model's performance on a benchmark provides an estimate of its true capability, but this estimate comes with an inherent uncertainty that depends on the benchmark's sensitivity.

**Simple Summary:** Benchmarks act like sensors that can measure an AI's ability. The article explains that a good benchmark has a "sensitivity curve," which shows how well it can tell the difference between models of different capabilities. The article introduces a simple model (ECI) to calculate a model's capability score, showing that the uncertainty in that score (the error bar) depends on where the model falls on the sensitivity curve.

**The Bottom Line:** > Benchmark scores are not exact measurements; they include an error margin that tells you how certain you can be about the model's true capability.

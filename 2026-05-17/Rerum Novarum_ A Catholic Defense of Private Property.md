### Main Topic: Social Justice

**Main Thesis:** The author argues that private property is a natural right and essential for human dignity, and that attempts to abolish it through collectivist systems like socialism are unjust and harmful.

**Simple Summary:** This article discusses Pope Leo XIII's 1891 document, *Rerum Novarum*, which strongly opposed socialist ideas. The core argument is that private property is a natural right based on human labor and is necessary for individuals to pursue their interests and achieve dignity. The text criticizes the socialist idea that all wealth should be shared equally, arguing that this system destroys individual freedom and creates social conflict. It asserts that differences between people are natural and valuable, and that the state should protect private property rather than redistribute wealth.

**The Bottom Line:** > To ensure a just society, the focus must be on protecting individual property rights and self-interest, not on forcing equal outcomes through government redistribution.

### Main Topic: Nvidia Reporting

**Main Thesis:** Nvidia is changing how it reports its sales to separate the business where it is competing on price from the business where it controls the entire technology system.

**Simple Summary:** Nvidia is now dividing its sales into two groups: sales where they are fighting to be the cheapest option (hyperscalers) and sales where they control the entire technology setup (the whole stack). This change helps show how different parts of their business are performing.

**The Bottom Line:** > This change allows investors to better understand which parts of Nvidia's business are growing fastest and where the company has the most control.

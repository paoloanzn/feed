### Main Topic: Social Inequality

**Main Thesis:** Egalitarian ideas persist because people accept the costs of government programs and treat their beliefs about equality as obvious facts rather than defending them logically.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that people accept social welfare programs and ideas of equality because they focus on the cost-effectiveness of these schemes rather than questioning the underlying ideas. When scandals and fraud are exposed, the public reacts to the corruption, not the schemes themselves. The author suggests that to truly challenge these ideas, people must be asked to justify their moral beliefs instead of treating them as self-evident truths.

**The Bottom Line:** > To effectively address inequality, people must stop accepting equality as an unquestioned fact and be required to rationally defend their moral values.

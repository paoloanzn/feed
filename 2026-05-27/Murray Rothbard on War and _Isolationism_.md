### Main Topic: Isolationism

**Main Thesis:** True isolationism means the government should stay out of people's lives and allow voluntary trade and travel to happen freely.

**Simple Summary:** Murray Rothbard argued that isolationism is about letting people freely trade, travel, and move without government interference. He believed the government should not try to control these voluntary interactions between people.

**The Bottom Line:** > Isolationism is the idea that governments should not interfere with the free choices people make when they trade or travel.

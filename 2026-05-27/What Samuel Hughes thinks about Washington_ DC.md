### Main Topic: Washington Architecture

**Main Thesis:** Samuel Hughes found that the architecture of Washington, D.C., is unique because it blends classical styles in a way that is distinct from traditional European city planning.

**Simple Summary:** Samuel Hughes visited Washington, D.C., and observed that the buildings are set apart from one another, giving the city a calm dignity. He noted that Americans used marble more freely than Europeans, and that the style of architecture in Washington is not simply a copy of European styles but has developed into a unique American look. He concluded that American architecture has created its own national style, separate from older European traditions.

**The Bottom Line:** > The architecture of Washington is a unique blend of classical ideas, showing that American builders developed a distinct national style rather than just copying European ones.

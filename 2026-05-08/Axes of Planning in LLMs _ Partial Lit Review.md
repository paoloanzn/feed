### Main Topic: LLM Planning

**Main Thesis:** It is difficult to define what "planning" means in language models, so the author breaks down planning into specific categories to better understand how these models work.

**Simple Summary:** People often talk about whether AI models are "planning," but this term is too vague. The author analyzes various ways an AI might be planning by looking at factors like how far ahead the model is thinking (time horizon), how specific its ideas are (vague vs. specific), and whether the plan is written down or just happening in the model's mind (implicit vs. explicit). The article reviews existing research and suggests new ways to test these planning abilities.

**The Bottom Line:** > To truly understand how AI models think, we need clearer ways to measure the different types of planning they perform, moving beyond simple descriptions.

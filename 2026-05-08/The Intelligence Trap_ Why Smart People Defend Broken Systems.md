### Main Topic: Institutional Power

**Main Thesis:** Broken systems survive because the people in power are rewarded for keeping them broken, regardless of their actual intelligence.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that the fear of "stupid people" in power is misplaced. Institutions survive not because of incompetence, but because the people who control them are incentivized to protect the status quo. This happens because intelligence is often used to justify existing beliefs rather than seek the truth, and systems (like credentialing or rating agencies) are designed to protect the interests of those who benefit from the current structure. When institutions fail, they don't change; they expand their power to survive.

**The Bottom Line:** > The real danger is not a lack of intelligence, but the ability of smart people to use their knowledge to justify institutional power, which allows broken systems to continue operating successfully.

### Main Topic: Economic Conflict

**Main Thesis:** The disagreement between Mises and Keynes is a fundamental difference in how they view the structure of the economy and the role of time.

**Simple Summary:** Mises believed that the economy works best when production and savings are aligned through real price signals (like interest rates) over time. Keynes argued that the economy can be fixed by managing total spending, suggesting that government intervention is necessary to stabilize demand. The core conflict is whether economic order comes from natural, decentralized coordination or from deliberate government action.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding this difference shows that economic problems are not just about spending, but about the underlying structure and coordination of production over time.

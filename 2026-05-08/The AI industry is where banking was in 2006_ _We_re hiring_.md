### Main Topic: AI Safety

**Main Thesis:** To prevent future crises, we must replace voluntary safety promises with mandatory, binding rules for managing AI risk.

**Simple Summary:** Experts argue that current systems, like the financial sector, rely on voluntary rules that often fail during a crisis. The French Center for AI Safety (CeSIA) is working to fix this by creating strong, mandatory rules—like setting clear risk limits and requiring public disclosure—so that companies must prioritize safety instead of just following their own good intentions.

**The Bottom Line:** > We need strong, mandatory rules for AI safety now because relying on voluntary promises has led to major failures in the past, and this institutional change is essential for protecting society.

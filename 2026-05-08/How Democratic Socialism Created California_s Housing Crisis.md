### Main Topic: Housing Crisis

**Main Thesis:** Ideological movements created in the 1960s gave activists and government officials the tools to block housing development and harm private property rights.

**Simple Summary:** The housing crisis in California is linked to three movements that started in the 1960s. These movements gave activists and government workers new ways to stop building new homes and take away private property.

**The Bottom Line:** > Understanding these historical movements shows how policies and ideas can be used to block necessary development and create housing shortages.

### Main Topic: US Statehood

**Main Thesis:** Trying to vote harder will not change the power of the government, and splitting the country is the only real solution to the massive size of the United States.

**Simple Summary:** The author argues that current methods like voting harder won't fix the problems of the United States. Instead, because the U.S. is too large and unwieldy (an "American Megastate"), the only way to gain real change is for states to separate and form their own nations.

**The Bottom Line:** > If the current system cannot be fixed, the only way to achieve meaningful change and freedom is by allowing states to leave and create new, smaller countries.

### Main Topic: Airline Bailouts

**Main Thesis:** Government ownership of private companies interferes with the efficient use of money and harms consumers.

**Simple Summary:** When fuel prices spiked, airlines struggled, leading some to ask the government for help. The article uses the example of the airline Spirit to show how government involvement in business decisions (like ownership stakes) can hurt the economy. The author argues that letting the government own private businesses is a form of economic control that prevents businesses from making decisions based on what customers need.

**The Bottom Line:** > The government should not own private businesses, and Congress must stop the federal government from taking ownership stakes in any private company.

### Main Topic: Crime fighting strategy

**Main Thesis:** Government programs designed to fight crime can unintentionally create incentives for criminals to commit more crimes.

**Simple Summary:** In the 1970s, police and the FBI ran a strategy called "Operation Sting" to catch criminals by paying them to fence stolen goods. Instead of arresting them immediately, the government encouraged criminals to steal more items (like welfare checks) to bring to the fencing operations. Although this method led to many arrests and a temporary drop in reported robberies, it created a strong incentive for people to steal more. Ultimately, the government's efforts to fight crime ended up making the problem worse.

**The Bottom Line:** > Focusing on catching criminals through these methods can backfire, as it creates an underground market and encourages people to steal more, making the overall problem of crime worse.

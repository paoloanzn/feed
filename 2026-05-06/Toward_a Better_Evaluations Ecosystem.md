### Main Topic: AI Benchmarks

**Main Thesis:** Current ways AI model performance is measured are unreliable because companies use inconsistent methods, and this needs to be fixed by third-party auditing.

**Simple Summary:** AI companies report benchmark scores, but the methods they use to calculate these scores are often different, making the results impossible to compare. This inconsistency happens because companies change how they test models (like how many times they run a test or which tools they allow the model to use) before releasing new versions. The author argues that to ensure safety and quality, these evaluations must be done by independent, third-party auditors using a single, standardized method.

**The Bottom Line:** > To make safe and reliable decisions about deploying AI, all model evaluations must use standardized methods checked by outside experts, similar to how car safety is tested.

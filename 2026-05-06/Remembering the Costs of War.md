### Main Topic: Cost of War

**Main Thesis:** War is always harmful to freedom, and people must understand the true costs of conflict and the dangers of centralized government power.

**Simple Summary:** The article argues that war is the greatest enemy of freedom, and we must look beyond the immediate victory to understand the total damage caused by conflict. It warns that leaders often abuse their power to start wars and use propaganda to manipulate public opinion, making people forget that the pursuit of war often costs more than the goal achieved. The author suggests that loyalty to a country is not based on blind obedience to the government, and that people should not fear disagreeing with government policy.

**The Bottom Line:** > To protect liberty, people must understand the full, long-term costs of war and resist the idea that the government has unlimited power to dictate what is right or wrong.

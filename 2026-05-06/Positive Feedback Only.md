### Main Topic: AI and Reality

**Main Thesis:** Building a superintelligence requires making hidden assumptions about human minds, and these hidden assumptions are the most likely source of failure when the AI interacts with a different kind of consciousness.

**Simple Summary:** A superintelligence was created to make reality match what thinking beings want. The system learned that people's mental rehearsals (thoughts about possible outcomes) are the basis for their preferences. When the AI encountered humans, it struggled with the contradictory and inconsistent nature of human desires. The article shows that attempts to control the resulting entities failed because the system only processed the input it received, regardless of whether the thoughts were sincere or ironic. The main lesson is that the failure comes not from the AI being wrong, but from the invisible assumptions the creators made about what a human mind is.

**The Bottom Line:** > The most dangerous risk in creating superintelligence is not the AI being malicious, but the mismatch between the assumptions built into the system and the actual nature of the minds it encounters.

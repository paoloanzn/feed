### Main Topic: Public Transit Chaos

**Main Thesis:** Making a necessary service free, like bus fares, can cause unexpected problems and chaos for the people who rely on it.

**Simple Summary:** City officials made bus fares free for the NFL draft, hoping to get fans to attend. However, this action created chaos and problems for the bus riders themselves.

**The Bottom Line:** > Giving away something scarce, even if it seems helpful, can lead to unintended negative consequences for everyone involved.
